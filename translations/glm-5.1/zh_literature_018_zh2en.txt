This text has been lost. Also, the Five Elements category records *Wuchengzi's Responses to Disasters and Anomalies* in fourteen volumes, and the Arts of the Bedchamber category records *Wuchengzi's Way of Yin* in thirty-six volumes, both of which are lost. Wucheng is a compound surname; his given name was Zhao, though some say it was Fu. Wang Fu of the Eastern Han dynasty, in his *Qianfu Lun: Zanxue* (Comments of a Recluse: In Praise of Learning), has the record that "Yao took Wucheng as his teacher."
