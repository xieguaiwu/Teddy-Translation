National Railway Single-Day Passenger Volume Hits Record

According to China State Railway Group Co., Ltd., national railways handled 21.24 million passenger trips on the first day of the May Day holiday, setting a new record for single-day passenger volume. The railway department added 1,200 passenger trains that day to fully meet passenger travel demands. The proportion of high-speed rail travel continued to rise, with an average of over 10,000 high-speed trains operating daily during the holiday. The 12306 railway ticketing system maintained stable operation during the peak period, with daily ticket sales exceeding 20 million at its highest point.

The quality of railway passenger services is also continuously improving. Electronic tickets have been fully implemented, and convenient services such as facial recognition for station entry and smart navigation are now available at more stations. High-speed rail dining has introduced local specialty foods and online ordering services. Passenger-friendly services like quiet carriages, nursing rooms, and barrier-free facilities are constantly being improved. The railway department is also advancing the development of municipal and intercity railways, promoting the integration of the four networks: trunk railways, intercity railways, suburban railways, and urban rail transit.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to witness more rapid and high-quality development. All sectors of society hold high expectations for this.
