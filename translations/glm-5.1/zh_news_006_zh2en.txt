China's Space Station Welcomes Fifth Batch of Scientific Experiment Projects

The China Manned Space Agency announced that the Tiangong Space Station has officially initiated its fifth batch of scientific experiment projects. This batch consists of 32 projects, covering multiple fields such as space life sciences, microgravity physics, space materials science, and astronomical observation, with international cooperation projects accounting for 40%. Research teams from 17 countries will conduct experiments via China's space station. To date, the Tiangong Space Station has completed over 200 scientific experiments, yielding a number of research outcomes with international impact.

The international cooperation model of China's space station has been positively recognized by the United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs. Through various forms such as joint experiments, mutual visits by astronauts, and data sharing, China's space station is emerging as a new platform for global space research collaboration. The station's expandable design also provides a technological verification platform for future deep-space exploration missions. Research and development of relevant technologies for the manned lunar landing mission is also advancing in parallel, with the goal of landing Chinese astronauts on the moon expected to be achieved within this decade.
