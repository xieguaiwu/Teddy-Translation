Shipments of China's Domestically Developed Chips See Significant Growth

Data released by the China Semiconductor Industry Association shows that domestic chip shipments reached 98 billion units in the first quarter of 2026, a year-on-year increase of 35%. Specifically, the domestic substitution rate for mature process chips has risen from approximately 15% in 2020 to over 40% at present. Several domestic chip design companies have made significant breakthroughs in niche markets such as the Internet of Things, power management, and radio frequency chips. Industry insiders state that although advanced processes still face challenges, China's chip industry has developed strong competitiveness in the mature process sector.

The rapid development of China's chip industry is also driven by its massive domestic demand market. The robust demand for chips in sectors such as new energy vehicles, industrial control, and smart homes has provided domestic chip companies with a broad platform for application verification. The continuous investment from the National Integrated Circuit Industry Investment Fund has also provided financial support for corporate research and development. Meanwhile, Chinese chip companies are actively participating in the formulation of international standards, enhancing their voice and influence in the global semiconductor industry.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have created a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening advancement of technological iteration and model innovation, related sectors are expected to witness faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society hold high expectations for this.
