China's Grain Output Remains Above 1.3 Trillion Jin for Eighth Consecutive Year

Data on the 2025 grain output released by the National Bureau of Statistics shows that the total national grain output reached 1.3982 trillion jin, remaining above 1.3 trillion jin for the eighth consecutive year. Both the planting area and yield of soybeans achieved growth, demonstrating significant results from expansion efforts. The contribution rate of agricultural science and technology progress rose to 65%, and the comprehensive mechanization rate for plowing, sowing, and harvesting reached 74%. A total of 1 billion mu of high-standard farmland has been developed, further consolidating the foundation of food security.

The foundation of food security lies in the protection of arable land and innovation in the seed industry. The national arable land area has remained stable at over 1.865 billion mu, and the construction of high-standard farmland continues to improve land quality. The action to revitalize the seed industry has been deeply implemented, with the promotion area of independently developed high-quality varieties expanding year by year. The green transformation of agriculture is also accelerating, with the use of chemical fertilizers and pesticides experiencing negative growth for consecutive years. The application of smart agricultural technologies has improved water resource utilization efficiency and the precision of pest and disease control. The grain storage and logistics system is continuously improving, reducing post-harvest losses.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening advancement of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to usher in faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society hold high expectations for this.
