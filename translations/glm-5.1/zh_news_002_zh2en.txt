Domestic Large Aircraft C919 Adds New International Routes

The Commercial Aircraft Corporation of China (COMAC) announced today that the domestic large aircraft C919 will add three new international routes starting next month, including Shanghai-Singapore, Beijing-Bangkok, and Guangzhou-Kuala Lumpur. As of now, 28 C919 aircraft have been delivered, accumulating over 100,000 safe flight hours, with the passenger load factor remaining above 85%. The type certification review by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) has also entered its final stage and is expected to be completed within the year. This marks a key step forward in the internationalization process of China's civil aviation manufacturing industry.

COMAC stated that the C919's internationalization strategy is not limited to route expansion, but also includes the construction of overseas maintenance networks and cooperation in pilot training. Several international aviation leasing companies have expressed purchase intentions for the C919. The industry believes that the C919's entry into the international market will change the landscape of the global narrow-body aircraft market, which has long been monopolized by the Boeing 737 and Airbus A320. The Civil Aviation Administration of China (CAAC) also stated that it will further strengthen cooperation with European and American aviation safety regulatory authorities to promote the safe operation of domestic civil aircraft globally.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that the relevant changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, with the in-depth advancement of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to usher in faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society hold high expectations for this.
