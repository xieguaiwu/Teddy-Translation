Full Implementation of New Curriculum Standards for Compulsory Education

The Ministry of Education announced that starting from the fall semester of 2026, the new compulsory education curriculum standards will be fully implemented in primary and secondary schools nationwide. The new curriculum standards focus on core competencies and increase the weighting of science, labor, and arts education. Notably, the starting grade for science classes has been adjusted from third grade to first grade, and the proportion of class hours for physical education and health has been increased to 10% to 11%. Textbook compilation and teacher training are already in full swing, and are expected to cover approximately 160 million primary and secondary school students.

The advancement of compulsory education reform also faces challenges such as balanced regional development and the building of the teaching workforce. School conditions in the central and western regions as well as rural areas are continuously improving, and teachers for subjects in short supply have been supplemented through policies such as the Special Post Plan and the Silver Age Lecture Plan. The strategic action for educational digitalization has promoted the joint development and sharing of high-quality educational resources. The continued implementation of the "Double Reduction" policy has alleviated the burden of students' homework and off-campus tutoring. Educational evaluation reform is also exploring the establishment of a more scientific and diversified evaluation system.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the landscape of industry development. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, with the deepening advancement of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to usher in more rapid and high-quality development. All sectors of society hold high expectations for this.
