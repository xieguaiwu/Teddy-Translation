Digital RMB Pilot Expanded Nationwide

The People's Bank of China announced that the digital RMB pilot will be expanded nationwide from the current 26 cities. As of May 2026, the cumulative transaction volume of the digital RMB has exceeded 2.5 trillion yuan, and the number of personal wallet accounts has surpassed 380 million. Cross-border payment pilots are also advancing, with interconnection mechanisms established with multiple countries and regions. The central bank's Digital Currency Research Institute stated that the promotion and application of the digital RMB will further enhance the efficiency and security of the payment system.

The promotion of the digital RMB is also gradually changing residents' payment habits. Compared with third-party payment platforms, the digital RMB possesses unique advantages such as controllable anonymity, dual offline payment, and programmability. The application of the digital RMB is being piloted and advanced in scenarios such as government subsidy distribution, cross-border trade settlement, and supply chain finance. The central bank stated that the digital RMB is positioned as a digital alternative to cash, which will not change the transmission mechanism of monetary policy, but will provide more tool options for precise regulation.
