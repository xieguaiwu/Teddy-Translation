China's Digital Economy Surpasses 60 Trillion Yuan in Scale

According to the "Digital China Development Report" recently released by the Cyberspace Administration of China, the scale of China's digital economy exceeded 60 trillion yuan in 2025, ranking second globally for the eighth consecutive year. The report notes that the deep integration of digital technologies with the real economy is reshaping the landscape of traditional industries. Among these developments, the application coverage rate of industrial internet platforms has reached 78%, while smart manufacturing demonstration projects have seen an average increase in production efficiency of 32%. Reforms for the market-oriented allocation of data elements are also progressing steadily, with the combined annual transaction volume of data exchanges in Beijing, Shanghai, Guangdong, and other regions surpassing 50 billion yuan.

Industry analysts note that the rapid development of the digital economy is reflected not only in scale expansion but also in structural optimization. Industrial digitalization now accounts for over 80% of the digital economy, and the digital transformation of traditional manufacturing is accelerating. The deep integration of next-generation information technologies such as 5G, artificial intelligence, and big data with the manufacturing sector has given rise to new models and formats, including smart factories, personalized customization, and collaborative manufacturing. The development of digital rural areas is also being advanced comprehensively, with application scenarios such as rural e-commerce and smart agriculture continuously expanding, gradually narrowing the urban-rural digital divide. Meanwhile, the legal and regulatory framework for data security and personal information protection continues to be refined.
