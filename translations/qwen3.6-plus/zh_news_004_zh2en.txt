China's Cumulative New Energy Vehicle Ownership Surpasses 40 Million

According to the latest data from the Ministry of Public Security, as of the end of May 2026, the national ownership of new energy vehicles (NEVs) has reached 41.5 million, accounting for 12.7% of the total vehicle fleet. In the first five months of 2026, 4.2 million NEVs were newly registered, representing a year-on-year increase of 45%. In terms of charging infrastructure, the total number of charging piles nationwide has reached 12 million, with the vehicle-to-charger ratio optimized to 3.5:1. Industry insiders believe that with advancements in battery technology and the improvement of charging networks, the market penetration rate of NEVs will exceed 30% by 2027.

The rapid development of the NEV industry has also driven coordinated progress across upstream and downstream supply chains. The energy density of power batteries continues to improve, while the costs of lithium iron phosphate (LFP) and ternary material batteries have dropped by approximately 60% and 50%, respectively. Next-generation battery technologies, such as solid-state and sodium-ion batteries, are also accelerating in research and development. The integration of intelligent connected vehicle technologies has significantly enhanced user experience in areas like autonomous driving and smart cockpits. The deployment of charging and battery-swapping infrastructure is expanding to highway service areas, townships, and residential communities.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these developments will have a profound impact on the landscape of the industry. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. Continuous policy optimization and positive market feedback have created a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening of technological iteration and business model innovation, the relevant sectors are expected to achieve even faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society look forward to this prospect with great anticipation.
