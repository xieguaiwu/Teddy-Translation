Total Number of 5G Base Stations Exceeds 5 Million

Data released by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology shows that as of April 2026, China's total number of 5G base stations has reached 5.12 million, covering all prefecture-level cities, county urban areas, and the vast majority of towns nationwide. The number of 5G users has surpassed 1.2 billion, with a penetration rate of 68%. Applications of 5G continue to deepen in fields such as the industrial internet, smart mining, and telemedicine, directly driving approximately 3.8 trillion yuan in total economic output. Research and development for 6G has also entered the critical technology breakthrough phase.

The integrated application of 5G technology is fostering new business models and industry structures. In the industrial sector, the deployment of 5G private networks has made scenarios such as remote equipment control, machine vision quality inspection, and unmanned patrols a reality. In healthcare, 5G-enabled remote surgery and mobile clinics have improved access to high-quality medical resources. In the cultural sector, 5G+8K ultra-high-definition live streaming and cloud-based performances have enriched people's cultural lives. Key focuses of 6G R&D include cutting-edge technologies such as terahertz communication, intelligent reflecting surfaces, and space-air-ground integrated networks.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these developments will have a profound impact on the industry landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have created a virtuous cycle. Over the next few years, with the deepening of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to experience faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society remain highly optimistic about this prospect.
