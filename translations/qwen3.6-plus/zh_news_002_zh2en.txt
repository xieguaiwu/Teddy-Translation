China's Homegrown C919 Large Passenger Aircraft to Add New International Routes

The Commercial Aircraft Corporation of China (COMAC) announced today that the domestically produced C919 large passenger aircraft will launch three new international routes starting next month, including Shanghai to Singapore, Beijing to Bangkok, and Guangzhou to Kuala Lumpur. To date, 28 C919 aircraft have been delivered, accumulating over 100,000 safe flight hours with a passenger load factor consistently above 85%. The type certification review by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) has also entered its final stage and is expected to be completed within this year. This marks a crucial step in the internationalization of China's civil aviation manufacturing industry.

COMAC stated that the C919's internationalization strategy extends beyond route expansion to include the development of overseas maintenance networks and pilot training partnerships. Several international aircraft leasing companies have already expressed interest in purchasing the C919. Industry observers believe that the C919's entry into the international market will disrupt the long-standing duopoly of the Boeing 737 and Airbus A320 in the global narrow-body aircraft sector. The Civil Aviation Administration of China (CAAC) also announced it will further strengthen cooperation with European and American aviation safety regulators to ensure the safe global operation of domestically produced civil aircraft.

This development has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts note that these changes will profoundly shape the future landscape of the sector. In the long term, all parties will need to strengthen coordination, jointly tackle challenges, and capitalize on emerging opportunities. Continuous policy optimization and positive market feedback have fostered a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, driven by technological advancements and innovative business models, the sector is expected to achieve faster and higher-quality growth. Society at large looks forward to this progress with great anticipation.
