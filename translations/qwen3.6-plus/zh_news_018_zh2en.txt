Record Single-Day Passenger Volume for China's Railways

According to China State Railway Group Co., Ltd., the national railway network handled 21.24 million passenger trips on the first day of the May Day holiday, setting a new historical record for single-day passenger volume. To fully meet travel demand, railway authorities added 1,200 passenger trains that day. The share of high-speed rail travel continues to grow, with an average of over 10,000 EMU train services operating daily during the holiday. The 12306 railway ticketing system operated smoothly during peak periods, with daily ticket sales peaking at over 20 million.

The quality of railway passenger services is also continuously improving. E-tickets have been fully adopted, and convenient services such as facial recognition for station entry and smart navigation are now available at more stations. High-speed rail catering has introduced local specialty foods and online ordering services. Passenger-friendly amenities such as quiet carriages, nursing rooms, and accessibility facilities continue to be enhanced. The railway sector is also advancing the development of suburban and intercity railways, promoting the integration of mainline railways, intercity railways, suburban railways, and urban rail transit into a unified four-network system.

This trend has drawn widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts note that these developments will have a profound impact on the industry's structural landscape. In the long run, all stakeholders need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have fostered a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening of technological upgrades and business model innovations, the sector is poised for even faster and higher-quality development. Society at large looks forward to this progress with great anticipation.
