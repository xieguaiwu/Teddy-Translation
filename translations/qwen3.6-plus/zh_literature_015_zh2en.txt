A literary and artistic concept proposed by the nineteenth-century French poet Théophile Gautier (see the preface to his novel *Mademoiselle de Maupin*). He believed that art could exist beyond all utilitarian purposes, and that the aim of creation lies in the artwork itself, independent of social or political concerns.
