China's Sports Industry Total Scale Surpasses 5 Trillion Yuan

Data released by the General Administration of Sport of China shows that in 2025, the total scale of China's sports industry reached 5.2 trillion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 15.8%. The national fitness movement continues to gain momentum, with the proportion of people who regularly participate in physical exercise reaching 38.5%. Over 800 marathon events were held throughout the year, with participation exceeding 10 million. The structure of sports consumption is also being optimized, with emerging business formats such as sports training, sports tourism, and smart sports equipment experiencing rapid growth.

The digital transformation of the sports industry is also accelerating. New formats like smart sports equipment, online fitness platforms, and e-sports are developing swiftly. Traditional sectors, including sports broadcasting rights, sports IP operations, and sports lotteries, continue to maintain steady growth. Following the elevation of national fitness to a national strategy, local governments have increased investment in sports infrastructure, leading to a steady rise in facilities such as community sports parks, fitness trails, and multi-purpose sports venues. Cross-sector collaboration models, such as the integration of sports with education and healthcare, are also being actively explored.

This trend has drawn widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts note that these developments will have a profound impact on the future landscape of the sector. In the long run, all stakeholders need to enhance coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market responses have created a virtuous cycle. Looking ahead, as technological upgrades and business model innovations deepen, related fields are poised for faster and higher-quality development. Society at large holds high expectations for this progress.
