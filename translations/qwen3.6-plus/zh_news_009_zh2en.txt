Significant Increase in Shipments of China's Independently Developed Chips

Data released by the China Semiconductor Industry Association shows that domestic chip shipments in the first quarter of 2026 reached 98 billion units, up 35% year-on-year. Among these, the domestic substitution rate for mature-process chips has climbed from approximately 15% in 2020 to over 40% currently. Several domestic chip design firms have achieved notable breakthroughs in niche segments such as the Internet of Things (IoT), power management, and radio frequency (RF) chips. Industry insiders note that while advanced processes still face challenges, China's chip industry has already built strong competitiveness in the mature-process sector.

The rapid development of China's chip industry is also fueled by strong domestic demand. Sustained high demand for chips in fields like new energy vehicles, industrial control, and smart homes has provided domestic companies with extensive platforms for application validation. Continuous funding from the National Integrated Circuit Industry Investment Fund has further supported corporate R&D efforts. At the same time, Chinese chip enterprises are actively participating in international standard-setting, steadily enhancing their voice and influence within the global semiconductor industry.

This trend has drawn widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these developments will profoundly reshape the industry landscape. Looking ahead, stakeholders must strengthen coordination and collaboration to jointly navigate challenges and capitalize on opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market responses have created a virtuous cycle. Over the next few years, as technological iteration and business model innovation deepen, related sectors are poised for faster, higher-quality growth. Society at large holds high expectations for this trajectory.
