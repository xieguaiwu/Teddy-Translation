Urban Renewal Initiatives Drive the Renovation of Old Residential Communities

According to the latest bulletin from the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, renovation work has been launched on a cumulative total of 280,000 old urban residential communities nationwide, benefiting over 65 million households. The 2026 plan targets the renovation of 53,000 such communities, with a focus on installing elevators, upgrading water, electricity, gas, and heating networks, and adding parking spaces and charging stations. Localities are also exploring new models to attract social capital for participation in both renovation and subsequent operations. Experts emphasize that urban renewal is not only a livelihood project but also a vital channel for stimulating domestic demand and driving economic growth.

During the renovation process, localities are also working to establish long-term management mechanisms. Post-renovation, communities are ensuring the sustainability of these upgrades by introducing professional property management, establishing homeowners' committees, and setting up maintenance funds. Integrating smart community development with neighborhood renovations has promoted the widespread adoption of facilities such as smart access control, intelligent parking systems, and automated waste sorting. Resident satisfaction surveys indicate that living comfort in renovated communities has improved by an average of over 40%.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts note that these developments will have a profound impact on the sector's landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have created a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening advancement of technological upgrades and innovative models, the relevant fields are expected to experience faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society hold high expectations for this progress.
