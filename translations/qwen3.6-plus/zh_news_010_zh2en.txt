Digital RMB Pilot Scope Expanded Nationwide

The People's Bank of China announced that the digital RMB pilot will be expanded from the current 26 cities to a nationwide scope. As of May 2026, the cumulative transaction volume of the digital RMB has exceeded 2.5 trillion yuan, with over 380 million personal wallets opened. Cross-border payment pilots are also advancing, with interconnection mechanisms established with multiple countries and regions. The PBOC's Digital Currency Research Institute stated that the promotion and application of the digital RMB will further enhance the efficiency and security of the payment system.

The rollout of the digital RMB is also gradually changing residents' payment habits. Compared with third-party payment platforms, the digital RMB offers unique advantages such as controllable anonymity, dual offline payment, and programmability. Pilot applications of the digital RMB are being advanced in scenarios such as government subsidy distribution, cross-border trade settlement, and supply chain finance. The PBOC clarified that the digital RMB is positioned as a digital replacement for cash and will not alter the transmission mechanism of monetary policy, but will provide more tools for precise regulation.
