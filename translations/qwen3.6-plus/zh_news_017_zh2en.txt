China's Cross-Border E-Commerce Trade Volume Hits a New High

Data released by the Ministry of Commerce shows that China's cross-border e-commerce import and export volume reached 2.8 trillion yuan in 2025, a year-on-year increase of 32%. Exported goods are primarily apparel, footwear, and hats, electronics, and home goods, while imports are dominated by beauty and skincare products, maternal and infant supplies, and food and health supplements. Growth in emerging markets such as Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Latin America has been particularly notable. The number of overseas warehouses has exceeded 2,500, covering major consumer markets worldwide. Cross-border e-commerce is emerging as a vital new engine for foreign trade growth.

The rapid development of cross-border e-commerce has also been driven by continuous improvements in logistics infrastructure and the international payment system. Companies such as Cainiao Network and JD Logistics have established efficient overseas distribution and delivery networks, reducing the delivery time for cross-border parcels from two weeks to just three to five days. Cross-border payment platforms now support multi-currency settlement and localized payment methods. The number of comprehensive cross-border e-commerce pilot zones has expanded to 165, yielding a series of replicable and scalable institutional innovations in customs clearance, taxation, and settlement.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these developments will have a profound impact on the industry's landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have created a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening of technological iteration and business model innovation, the sector is expected to achieve even faster and higher-quality development. Society at large holds high expectations for this progress.
