China's Grain Output Remains Stable Above 1.3 Trillion Jin for Eighth Consecutive Year

Data on grain production for 2025 released by the National Bureau of Statistics shows that the country's total grain output reached 1.3982 trillion jin, marking the eighth consecutive year it has remained stable above 1.3 trillion jin. Both the planting area and yield of soybeans achieved growth, demonstrating notable results from the expansion of soybean cultivation. The contribution rate of agricultural scientific and technological progress rose to 65%, and the comprehensive mechanization rate for crop cultivation, planting, and harvesting reached 74%. Cumulative construction of high-standard farmland has reached 1 billion mu, further solidifying the foundation for food security.

The foundation of food security lies in cultivated land protection and seed industry innovation. The national cultivated land area has been stabilized at over 1.865 billion mu, while the development of high-standard farmland continues to improve land quality. The seed industry revitalization initiative has been deeply implemented, with the promotion area of independently developed superior varieties expanding year by year. The green transition of agriculture is also accelerating, as the use of chemical fertilizers and pesticides has declined for multiple consecutive years. The application of smart agriculture technologies has improved water use efficiency and the precision of pest and disease control. The grain storage and logistics system is continuously being improved, effectively reducing post-harvest losses.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the development landscape of the sector. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening advancement of technological upgrades and innovative models, related fields are expected to experience faster and higher-quality development. Society at large looks forward to these developments with great anticipation.
