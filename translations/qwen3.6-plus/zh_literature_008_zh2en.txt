Outside the black cloak, the cold grew sharper, piercing the nerves of my face like awls. Yet inside that cloak, within this space of black and white, it was as warm as spring. She no longer carried the scent of newly harvested rice; instead, the air was filled with the fragrance of the Milan flower—a bloom whose sweetness borders on bitterness. To me, this black-and-white space, the stark contrast of hot and cold, combined with a flower sweet to the point of bitterness, was what I called "sex." Unwilling to let her vanish again, I gripped her wrist tightly... She straightened her body, let her white shoulders slip out from the cloak, and licked her lips.
