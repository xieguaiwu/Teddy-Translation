Major breakthroughs in deep-sea exploration technology

The China Ocean Mineral Resources Association announced that the manned submersible Fendouzhe (Striver) completed its 100th dive in the Mariana Trench in the Pacific Ocean, reaching a maximum depth of 10,909 meters. At the same time, China's independently developed deep-sea equipment, such as deep-sea drilling vessels and seabed observation networks, are also steadily advancing. The technological reserves for the exploration and development of deep-sea mineral resources have entered the world's forefront. Scientists state that deep-sea research holds irreplaceable scientific value for understanding Earth's evolution, climate change, and the origin of life.

Advances in deep-sea exploration technology also provide new means for the development of marine resources and the protection of the marine environment. Deep-sea mineral resources mainly include polymetallic nodules, cobalt-rich crusts, and hydrothermal sulfides, which contain strategic metal elements such as nickel, cobalt, copper, and manganese. China holds several exclusive exploration contract areas in international seabed regions. Research on deep-sea ecological environments is also advancing simultaneously, with scientists using deep-sea detectors to conduct long-term observations of special ecosystems such as hydrothermal vents and cold seeps, discovering a large number of new species.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that the relevant changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, with the deepening of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to usher in faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are full of expectations for this.
