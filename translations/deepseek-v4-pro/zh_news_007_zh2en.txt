**Urban Renewal Initiatives Drive Renovation of Old Residential Communities**

According to the latest report from the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, renovation of old urban residential communities nationwide has started in a cumulative total of 280,000 communities, benefiting over 65 million households. The plan for 2026 targets the renovation of 53,000 old communities, with a focus on installing elevators, upgrading water, electricity, gas and heating networks, and adding parking spaces and charging piles. Localities are also exploring new models to attract private capital into renovation and operation. Experts note that urban renewal is not only a project that improves people's livelihoods, but also an important means of boosting domestic demand and driving economic growth.

During the renovation of old communities, localities are also exploring the establishment of long-term management mechanisms. Renovated communities introduce professional property management, establish homeowners’ committees, and set up maintenance funds to ensure the sustainability of the improvements. The integration of smart community construction with old community renovation has driven the widespread adoption of facilities such as smart access control, smart parking, and smart waste sorting. Resident satisfaction surveys show that living comfort in renovated communities has improved by over 40% on average.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that the related changes will have a profound impact on the sector’s development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have created a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation advance further, the relevant fields are expected to achieve faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society look forward to this with great anticipation.
