*Wuchengzi*
Already lost. In the bibliography of the Five Elements school, there was recorded *Wuchengzi's Responses to Disasters and Anomalies* in fourteen volumes; in the school of the Art of the Bedchamber, *Wuchengzi's Way of Yin* in thirty-six volumes, all now lost. Wucheng is a two-character surname, given name Zhao, or alternatively, Fu. In Wang Fu's *Qianfu Lun: Zaxue* from the Eastern Han, there is a record that "Yao took Wucheng as his teacher."
