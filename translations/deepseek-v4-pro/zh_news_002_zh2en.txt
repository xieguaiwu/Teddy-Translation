China's Domestically Developed C919 Aircraft Adds International Routes

The Commercial Aircraft Corporation of China (COMAC) announced today that the domestically developed C919 will add three new international routes starting next month, including Shanghai to Singapore, Beijing to Bangkok, and Guangzhou to Kuala Lumpur. To date, 28 C919s have been delivered, accumulating over 100,000 hours of safe flight with a passenger load factor consistently above 85%. The type certification review by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) has also entered its final stage and is expected to be completed within the year. This marks a critical step in the internationalization of China's civil aviation manufacturing industry.

COMAC stated that the C919's internationalization strategy extends beyond route expansion to include the establishment of overseas maintenance networks and pilot training cooperation. Several international aircraft leasing companies have expressed interest in purchasing the C919. The industry believes that the C919's entry into the global market will reshape a landscape long dominated by the Boeing 737 and Airbus A320 in the narrow-body passenger aircraft segment. The Civil Aviation Administration of China also indicated it will further strengthen cooperation with European and American aviation safety regulators to promote the safe operation of domestically produced civil aircraft worldwide.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts note that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development pattern. In the long term, all parties need to enhance coordination and collaboration, jointly address challenges, and seize opportunities. Continuous policy optimization and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation advance further, related fields are expected to experience faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are full of expectations for this.
