World's Largest Offshore Wind Project Connects to Grid

The world's largest offshore wind project, constructed by Chinese enterprises, has achieved full-capacity grid connection in the waters off Yangjiang, Guangdong. The project has a total installed capacity of 6.5 gigawatts and an annual electricity output of approximately 260 billion kilowatt-hours, enough to meet the yearly power needs of around 9 million households. The project utilized multiple domestically produced large-capacity wind turbines, reducing construction costs. Offshore wind power, as a key component of clean energy, is becoming a crucial pillar in the transformation of China's energy structure.

The construction of offshore wind projects has also driven the export of related equipment manufacturing and engineering technology services. Chinese-made wind turbines and installation vessels have been exported to multiple countries and regions, including Europe and Southeast Asia. Integrated development models combining offshore wind with marine aquaculture, seawater hydrogen production, and wave energy power generation are also being explored. Breakthroughs have been made in deep-sea floating wind technology, laying the foundation for tapping into wind energy resources in deeper and more distant waters. The rapid growth of the offshore wind power industry provides vital support for China's efforts to meet its carbon peak and carbon neutrality goals.

This trend has drawn widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the sector's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and collaboration to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous improvement of policies and positive market feedback have formed a constructive interplay. In the coming years, as technological iterations and model innovations advance further, related fields are expected to experience faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are looking forward to this with great anticipation.
