She no longer gave off the scent of new rice but exuded the fragrance of michelia. Michelia is a flower with a sweetness so sharp it turns bitter. To me, that black-and-white space, the stark contrast of cold and heat, and the sweetly bitter flower together meant "sex." Unwilling to let her vanish again, I tightly grasped her wrist...
