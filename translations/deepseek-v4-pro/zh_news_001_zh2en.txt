China's Digital Economy Surpasses 60 Trillion Yuan

According to the "Digital China Development Report" recently released by the Cyberspace Administration of China, China's digital economy will exceed 60 trillion yuan in 2025, ranking second globally for eight consecutive years. The report points out that the deep integration of digital technology and the real economy is reshaping traditional industrial patterns. Among them, the application coverage rate of industrial internet platforms has reached 78%, and the production efficiency of intelligent manufacturing demonstration projects has increased by an average of 32%. The market-oriented allocation reform of data as a factor of production is also steadily advancing, with the annual transaction volume of data exchanges in Beijing, Shanghai, Guangdong and other places exceeding 50 billion yuan.

Industry insiders point out that the rapid development of the digital economy is reflected not only in scale growth but also in structural optimization. The share of industrial digitalization in the digital economy has exceeded 80%, and the digital transformation of traditional manufacturing is accelerating. The deep integration of next-generation information technologies such as 5G, artificial intelligence, and big data with manufacturing has given rise to new models and new business forms like smart factories, customized production, and collaborative manufacturing. Digital village construction is being advanced comprehensively, with application scenarios such as rural e-commerce and smart agriculture constantly expanding, gradually narrowing the urban-rural digital divide. The legal and regulatory framework for data security and personal information protection is also continuously improving.
