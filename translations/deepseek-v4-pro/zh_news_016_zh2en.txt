The total number of 5G base stations surpasses 5 million

According to data released by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, as of April 2026, the total number of 5G base stations in China had reached 5.12 million, covering all prefecture-level cities, county urban areas, and the vast majority of townships. The number of 5G users exceeded 1.2 billion, with a penetration rate of 68%. The application of 5G in fields such as the industrial internet, smart mining, and telemedicine continues to deepen, with 5G directly driving total economic output of approximately 3.8 trillion yuan. 6G research and development has also entered the stage of tackling key technologies.

The integrated application of 5G technology is giving rise to new business models and industrial forms. In the industrial sector, the use of 5G private networks has made scenarios such as remote equipment control, machine vision quality inspection, and unmanned patrol inspection a reality. In the medical field, 5G-enabled remote surgery and mobile diagnosis and treatment have improved the accessibility of high-quality medical resources. In the cultural sector, 5G+8K ultra-high-definition video live streaming and cloud performing arts have enriched people's cultural lives. Key areas of 6G research and development include cutting-edge technologies such as terahertz communication, intelligent metasurfaces, and integrated space-ground networks.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts note that the relevant changes will have a profound impact on the development landscape of the sector. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation, jointly address challenges, and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the in-depth advancement of technological iteration and model innovation, the relevant fields are expected to experience faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are full of anticipation for this.
