China's grain output has remained stable above 1.3 trillion jin for eight consecutive years

Data on 2025 grain output released by the National Bureau of Statistics show that the country's total grain output reached 1.3982 trillion jin, staying above 1.3 trillion jin for the eighth year in a row. Soybean acreage and production both achieved growth, with notable results from expanded planting. The contribution rate of advances in agricultural science and technology rose to 65%, and the comprehensive mechanization rate for crop cultivation and harvesting reached 74%. A cumulative total of 1 billion mu of high-standard farmland has been developed, further strengthening the foundation of food security.

The foundation of food security lies in farmland protection and innovation in the seed industry. The country's arable land area has been stabilized at over 1.865 billion mu, while the development of high-standard farmland continues to enhance land quality. The seed industry revitalization campaign is being pushed forward in depth, and the area sown with independently developed high-quality varieties is expanding year by year. The green transformation of agriculture is also accelerating, with the use of chemical fertilizers and pesticides recording negative growth for several consecutive years. The application of smart farming technologies has improved water-use efficiency and the precision of pest and disease control. The grain storage and logistics system is being steadily improved, reducing post-harvest losses.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts note that these changes will have a profound impact on the development landscape of the sector. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination, jointly address challenges, and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have created a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation advance further, related fields are expected to embrace faster, higher-quality development. All sectors of society are full of expectation for this.
