China’s biomedical innovation achievements surge in concentrated fashion  

The National Medical Products Administration announced that 48 innovative drugs were approved for market in 2025, a record high. Domestic innovative drugs accounted for 60%, covering multiple therapeutic areas including oncology, autoimmune diseases, and rare diseases. The regulatory framework for cutting-edge fields such as cell therapy and gene therapy is also gradually improving. A number of Chinese biomedical enterprises have achieved internationally competitive R&D results in areas like bispecific antibodies and ADC drugs, with the value of license-out deals continuing to grow.

The innovative development of the biomedical industry cannot be separated from the ongoing optimization of the policy environment. The reform of the national drug review and approval system has advanced in depth, significantly accelerating the speed at which innovative drugs reach the market. Medical insurance negotiations and centralized volume-based procurement have not only lowered drug prices but also provided market access channels for innovative drugs. The full implementation of the marketing authorization holder system has invigorated the drive for R&D innovation. Biomedical industry parks are flourishing across the country, forming distinctive industrial clusters.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts note that these changes will have a profound impact on the landscape of the sector. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and collaboration, jointly address challenges, and seize opportunities. The continuous refinement of policies and positive market feedback have created a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, as technology iteration and model innovation deepen, related fields are expected to embrace faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are full of expectations for this.
