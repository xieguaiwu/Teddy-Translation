China's Sports Industry Total Scale Exceeds 5 Trillion Yuan

Data released by the General Administration of Sport of China shows that in 2025, China's sports industry total scale reached 5.2 trillion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 15.8%. The national fitness campaign continues to heat up, with the proportion of people regularly participating in physical exercise reaching 38.5%. More than 800 marathon events were held throughout the year, with over 10 million participations. The sports consumption structure is also optimizing, with rapid growth in emerging sectors such as sports training, sports tourism, and smart sports equipment.

The digital transformation of the sports industry is also accelerating. New formats such as smart sports equipment, online fitness platforms, and e-sports are developing rapidly. Traditional businesses like sports event broadcasting rights, sports IP operations, and sports lotteries are also maintaining stable growth. After national fitness became a national strategy, localities increased investment in sports infrastructure, with more community sports parks, fitness trails, multi-purpose sports fields, and other facilities. Cross-sector cooperation models such as integration of sports and education and integration of sports and medicine are also being explored.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the development pattern of the industry. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination, jointly address challenges, and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, with the deepening of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to usher in even faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are full of expectations for this.
