China's Forest Coverage Rate Rises to 25.3%

The latest data released by the National Forestry and Grassland Administration shows that the national forest coverage rate has increased to 25.3%, with forest area reaching 245 million hectares. Over the past decade, China has added more than 60 million hectares of forest, making it the country with the largest increase in forest resources globally. The Three-North Shelterbelt Forest Program, the Grain for Green Program, and the national land greening campaign have been steadily advanced. International environmental organizations point out that China's afforestation efforts have made substantial contributions to global carbon emission reduction and biodiversity conservation.

The service function value of forest ecosystems is also continuously improving. The forest carbon sink trading market is gradually being refined, and forestry carbon sink projects provide new income channels for people in forest areas. The pilot reform of the national park system has been deepened, and a nature reserve system with national parks as the mainstay is taking shape. A comprehensive natural forest protection and restoration system has been established, with forest resource management and protection responsibilities assigned to individuals. Green industries such as under-forest economy, forest tourism, and forest wellness are thriving, achieving a win-win situation of ecological protection and economic development.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that the related changes will have a profound impact on the development landscape of the industry. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation deepen, the relevant fields are expected to experience faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are full of expectations for this.
