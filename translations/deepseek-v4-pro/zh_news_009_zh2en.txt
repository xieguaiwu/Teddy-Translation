Shipments of Domestically Developed Chips in China Surge

Data released by the China Semiconductor Industry Association shows that domestic chip shipments reached 98 billion units in the first quarter of 2026, a year-on-year increase of 35 percent. Among these, the domestic substitution rate for mature process chips has risen from approximately 15 percent in 2020 to over 40 percent currently. Several domestic chip design companies have achieved significant breakthroughs in niche markets such as the Internet of Things, power management, and radio frequency chips. Industry insiders note that although advanced processes still face challenges, China's chip industry has formed strong competitiveness in the mature process sector.

The rapid development of China's chip industry also benefits from the pull of its huge domestic market. Sustained strong demand for chips in areas such as new energy vehicles, industrial control, and smart homes provides domestic chip enterprises with broad application verification platforms. Continuous investment from the National Integrated Circuit Industry Investment Fund has also provided financial support for corporate research and development. Meanwhile, Chinese chip companies are actively participating in the formulation of international standards to enhance their voice and influence in the global semiconductor industry.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that the relevant changes will profoundly impact the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to address challenges and seize opportunities together. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interactive cycle. In the coming years, with deepening technological iteration and model innovation, related sectors are expected to embrace more rapid and high-quality development. All sectors of society are full of expectations for this.
