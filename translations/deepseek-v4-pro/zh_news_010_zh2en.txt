Digital Yuan Pilot Program Expanded Nationwide

The People's Bank of China announced that the digital yuan pilot will be expanded from the current 26 cities to the whole country. As of May 2026, the cumulative transaction volume of digital yuan has exceeded 2.5 trillion yuan, and the number of personal wallet accounts has surpassed 380 million. Cross-border payment trials are also progressing, with interconnectivity mechanisms established with multiple countries and regions. The Digital Currency Research Institute of the central bank stated that the promotion and application of digital yuan will further enhance the efficiency and security of the payment system.

The spread of digital yuan is also gradually changing residents' payment habits. Compared with third-party payment platforms, digital yuan offers unique advantages such as controllable anonymity, dual offline payment, and programmability. In scenarios like government subsidy distribution, cross-border trade settlement, and supply chain finance, the application of digital yuan is being piloted and advanced. The central bank said that digital yuan is positioned as a digital replacement for cash, which will not alter the transmission mechanism of monetary policy but will provide more instrument options for precise regulation.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the development landscape of the sector.
