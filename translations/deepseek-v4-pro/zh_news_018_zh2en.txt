National Railway Sets Single-Day Passenger Volume Record

According to China State Railway Group Co., Ltd., on the first day of the May Day holiday, national railways transported 21.24 million passengers, setting a new all-time high for single-day passenger volume. The railway department added 1,200 passenger trains that day to fully meet travel demand. The share of high-speed rail trips continued to rise, with an average of over 10,000 EMU trains operating daily during the holiday. The railway's 12306 system maintained stable operation during peak periods, with daily ticket sales exceeding 20 million at their highest.

The quality of railway passenger services is also steadily improving. Electronic ticketing has been fully popularized, and convenient services such as facial recognition entry and smart navigation now cover more stations. High-speed rail dining has introduced local specialty foods and online ordering services. Humanized features such as quiet carriages, mother-and-baby rooms, and barrier-free facilities continue to be enhanced. The railway department is also promoting the development of suburban and intercity railways, advancing the integration of trunk railways, intercity railways, suburban railways, and urban rail transit into a four-network system.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts note that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination, jointly address challenges, and seize opportunities. Continuous policy optimization and positive market feedback have created a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation deepen, the relevant fields are expected to achieve faster and higher-quality development, and all sectors of society are filled with anticipation.
