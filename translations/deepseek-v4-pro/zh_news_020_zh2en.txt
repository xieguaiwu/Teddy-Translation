New Curriculum Standards for Compulsory Education Fully Implemented

The Ministry of Education announced that starting from the fall semester of 2026, the new compulsory education curriculum standards will be fully implemented in primary and secondary schools nationwide. The new standards focus on cultivating core competencies and increase the emphasis on science, labor, and arts education. Among the adjustments, the starting grade for science classes has been moved from third grade to first grade, and the proportion of class hours for physical education and health has been raised to 10% to 11%. Textbook compilation and teacher training are already underway in full swing, and are expected to cover approximately 160 million primary and secondary school students.

The advancement of compulsory education reform also faces challenges such as balanced regional development and the building of the teaching workforce. School conditions in central and western regions and rural schools are being continuously improved, and teachers in understaffed subjects are being supplemented through policies such as the Special-Post Teacher Plan and the Silver Age Lecture Plan. The strategic initiative for education digitalization is promoting the co-construction and sharing of high-quality educational resources. The sustained implementation of the “double reduction” policy has lightened students’ homework burden and off-campus tutoring load. Education evaluation reform is also exploring the establishment of a more scientific and diversified evaluation system.

This trend has drawn widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the development landscape of the sector. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation advance in depth, relevant fields are expected to experience faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are full of expectations for this.
