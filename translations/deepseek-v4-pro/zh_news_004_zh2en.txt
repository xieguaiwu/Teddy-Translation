China's cumulative ownership of new energy vehicles surpasses 40 million

According to the latest data from the Ministry of Public Security, as of the end of May 2026, the total number of new energy vehicles in China had reached 41.5 million, accounting for 12.7% of all vehicles. In the first five months of 2026, 4.2 million new energy vehicles were newly registered, a year-on-year increase of 45%. In terms of charging infrastructure, the total number of charging piles nationwide reached 12 million, with the vehicle-to-pile ratio improving to 3.5:1. Industry insiders believe that with advances in battery technology and the improvement of charging networks, the market penetration rate of new energy vehicles will exceed 30% by 2027.

The rapid development of the new energy vehicle industry has also driven coordinated progress in upstream and downstream industrial chains. The energy density of power batteries continues to improve, with the costs of lithium iron phosphate and ternary material batteries falling by about 60% and 50%, respectively. Next-generation battery technologies such as solid-state batteries and sodium-ion batteries are also accelerating in research and development. The integrated application of intelligent connected technologies has significantly enhanced user experience in areas such as autonomous driving and smart cockpits for new energy vehicles. The layout of charging and battery-swapping infrastructure is extending to highway service areas, townships, and residential communities.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the development landscape of the sector. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and collaboration, jointly address challenges, and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, with the deepening advancement of technological iteration and model innovation, the relevant fields are expected to achieve faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are full of anticipation.
