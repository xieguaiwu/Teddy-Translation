China's High-Speed Rail Mileage Surpasses 55,000 Kilometers

According to data released by China State Railway Group, the operating mileage of China's high-speed rail has exceeded 55,000 kilometers, accounting for more than two-thirds of the global total. In 2026, approximately 2,500 kilometers of new high-speed rail lines are planned, including major projects such as the Chongqing–Kunming High-Speed Railway and the Xi'an–Chengdu High-Speed Railway. The continuous expansion of the high-speed rail network is profoundly reshaping regional economic patterns, with commuting times between city clusters such as Beijing–Tianjin–Hebei, the Yangtze River Delta, and the Guangdong–Hong Kong–Macao Greater Bay Area significantly shortened. The share of high-speed rail passenger flow in total railway passenger volume has risen to 75%.

The economic effects of the high-speed rail network are extending beyond transportation into broader regional economies. The development of new urban areas, industrial parks, and commercial facilities around high-speed rail stations is constantly improving, forming so-called high-speed rail economic belts. The tourism industry has benefited notably, with significant growth in tourism revenue for small and medium-sized cities along high-speed rail routes. High-speed rail freight services are also being explored, offering rapid transportation services for high-value-added goods. Research and development on high-speed maglev trains reaching speeds of 400 kilometers per hour has entered the test verification phase.

This trend has triggered widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts note that such changes will have a profound impact on the sector's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination, jointly address challenges, and seize opportunities. Continuous policy optimization and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, with deeper technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to achieve faster and higher-quality development, a prospect that society as a whole eagerly anticipates.
