Digital yuan pilot expanded nationwide

The People's Bank of China has announced that the digital yuan pilot will be expanded from the current 26 cities to cover the entire country. As of May 2026, the cumulative transaction volume of digital yuan has exceeded 2.5 trillion yuan, with over 380 million personal wallet accounts opened. Cross-border payment trials are also advancing, with interconnection mechanisms established with multiple countries and regions. The Digital Currency Research Institute of the central bank stated that the promotion and application of digital yuan will further enhance the efficiency and security of the payment system.

The promotion of digital yuan is also gradually changing residents' payment habits. Compared with third-party payment platforms, digital yuan offers unique advantages such as controllable anonymity, dual offline payment, and programmability. In scenarios such as government subsidy distribution, cross-border trade settlement, and supply chain finance, the application of digital yuan is being piloted and advanced. The central bank stated that digital yuan is positioned as a digital replacement for cash and will not alter the transmission mechanism of monetary policy, but it will provide more tools for precise regulation.
