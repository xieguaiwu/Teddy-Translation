China's cross-border e-commerce trade volume reaches new high

Data released by the Ministry of Commerce shows that in 2025, China's cross-border e-commerce import and export volume reached 2.8 trillion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 32%. Export goods are mainly clothing, shoes, hats, electronic products, and household items, while imports are mainly beauty and skincare, maternal and baby products, and food and health products. Growth in emerging markets such as Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Latin America is particularly significant. The number of overseas warehouses exceeds 2,500, covering major global consumer markets. Cross-border e-commerce is becoming an important new engine for foreign trade growth.

The rapid development of cross-border e-commerce also benefits from the continuous improvement of logistics infrastructure and the perfection of the international payment system. Companies such as Cainiao Network and JD Logistics have established efficient sorting and delivery networks overseas, shortening the delivery time of cross-border parcels from two weeks to three to five days. Cross-border payment platforms support settlement in multiple currencies and localized payment methods. The comprehensive pilot zones for cross-border e-commerce have been expanded to 165, producing a batch of replicable institutional innovations in customs clearance, taxation, and settlement.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that the relevant changes will have a profound impact on the development pattern of the industry. In the long term, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, with the deepening of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to usher in faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are full of expectations for this.
