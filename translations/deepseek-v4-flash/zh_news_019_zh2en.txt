China's Biomedical Innovation Achievements Surge

The National Medical Products Administration announced that in 2025, a total of 48 innovative drugs were approved for market, setting a record high. Among them, domestically developed innovative drugs accounted for 60%, covering multiple therapeutic areas such as oncology, autoimmune diseases, and rare diseases. Regulatory systems for cutting-edge fields like cell therapy and gene therapy are also being gradually improved. Several Chinese biomedical companies have achieved internationally competitive R&D results in areas such as bispecific antibodies and antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs), with the value of license-out deals continuing to grow.

The innovation and development of the biomedical industry cannot be separated from the continuous optimization of the policy environment. The reform of the national drug review and approval system has been deepened, significantly accelerating the time-to-market for innovative drugs. Medical insurance negotiations and volume-based procurement have not only lowered drug prices but also provided market access channels for innovative drugs. The full implementation of the drug marketing authorization holder system has stimulated R&D and innovation vitality. The construction of biomedical industrial parks is booming across the country, forming distinctive industrial clusters.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the development landscape of the sector. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and collaboration to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with deeper technological iteration and model innovation, relevant fields are expected to achieve faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are filled with expectations for this.
