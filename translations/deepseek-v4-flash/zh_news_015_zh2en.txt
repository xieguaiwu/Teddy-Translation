China's grain output has remained stable at over 1.3 trillion jin for eight consecutive years

According to the 2025 grain output data released by the National Bureau of Statistics, the total national grain output reached 1.3982 trillion jin, remaining stable at over 1.3 trillion jin for the eighth consecutive year. Soybean planting area and output both increased, with significant expansion achievements. The contribution rate of agricultural scientific and technological progress rose to 65%, and the comprehensive mechanization rate of crop cultivation and harvesting reached 74%. The cumulative construction of high-standard farmland has reached 1 billion mu, further solidifying the foundation of food security.

The foundation of food security lies in farmland protection and seed industry innovation. The national farmland area remains stable at over 1.865 billion mu, with high-standard farmland construction continuously improving land quality. The seed industry revitalization initiative has been deeply implemented, with the promotion area of independently developed superior varieties expanding year by year. The green transformation of agriculture is also accelerating, with the use of chemical fertilizers and pesticides achieving negative growth for many consecutive years. The application of smart agricultural technologies has improved water resource utilization efficiency and the precision of pest control. The grain storage and logistics system is continuously being improved to reduce post-harvest losses.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that relevant changes will have a profound impact on the development landscape of the sector. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, with the deepening advancement of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to usher in faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are full of expectations for this.
