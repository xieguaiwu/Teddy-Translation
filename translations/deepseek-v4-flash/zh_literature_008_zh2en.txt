She no longer exuded the fragrance of new rice, but instead the scent of Milan. Milan is a flower whose sweetness borders on bitterness. To me, that black-and-white space, the stark contrast of hot and cold, and the sweet-yet-bitter flower—that is what "sex" means. I wouldn't let her disappear again, so I gripped her wrist tightly... Then she straightened up, thrust her white shoulders out from under the cloak, and licked her lips. No matter what, the second time passed as naturally as flowing water.
