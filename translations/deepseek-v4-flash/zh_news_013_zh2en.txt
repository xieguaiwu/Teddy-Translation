China's sports industry total scale exceeds 5 trillion yuan

Data released by the General Administration of Sport of China shows that in 2025, the total scale of China's sports industry reached 5.2 trillion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 15.8%. The national fitness movement continues to heat up, with the proportion of people who regularly engage in physical exercise reaching 38.5%. Over 800 marathon events were held throughout the year, with more than 10 million participants. The structure of sports consumption is also being optimized, with emerging sectors such as sports training, sports tourism, and smart sports equipment growing rapidly.

The digital transformation of the sports industry is also accelerating. New business forms such as smart sports equipment, online fitness platforms, and e-sports are developing rapidly. Traditional businesses like sports event broadcasting rights, sports IP operations, and sports lottery are also maintaining stable growth. After national fitness was elevated to a national strategy, local governments have increased investment in sports infrastructure, with facilities such as community sports parks, fitness trails, and multi-functional sports venues continuously expanding. Cross-sector cooperation models such as the integration of sports and education, and sports and medicine, are also being explored.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the development landscape of the sector. In the long term, all parties need to strengthen coordination and collaboration to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to usher in faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are full of anticipation.
