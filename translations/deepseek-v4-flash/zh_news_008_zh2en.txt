China's high-speed rail mileage exceeds 55,000 kilometers

According to data released by China State Railway Group, the operating mileage of China's high-speed rail has exceeded 55,000 kilometers, accounting for more than two-thirds of the world's total high-speed rail mileage. In 2026, it is planned to build about 2,500 kilometers of new high-speed rail lines, including major projects such as the Chongqing-Kunming High-Speed Railway and the Xi'an-Chengdu High-Speed Railway. The continuous expansion of the high-speed rail network is profoundly changing the regional economic landscape, significantly shortening commuting times between city clusters such as the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region, the Yangtze River Delta, and the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area. The proportion of high-speed rail passenger traffic in total railway passenger volume has risen to 75%.

The economic effects of the high-speed rail network are permeating from the transportation sector into the broader regional economy. The development of new urban areas around high-speed rail stations, the construction of industrial parks, and the improvement of commercial facilities are continuously advancing, forming so-called high-speed rail economic belts. The tourism industry has benefited particularly significantly, with small and medium-sized cities along high-speed rail lines seeing notable increases in tourism revenue. High-speed rail freight services are also being explored, offering rapid transportation for high-value-added goods. Research and development of high-speed maglev trains with a speed of 400 km/h have also entered the testing and verification stage.

This trend has attracted widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that related changes will have a profound impact on the development pattern of the industry. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, with the deepening of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to usher in faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are full of expectations for this.
