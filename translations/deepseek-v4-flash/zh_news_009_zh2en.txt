Shipments of China's self-developed chips surge significantly

Data released by the China Semiconductor Industry Association shows that in the first quarter of 2026, domestic chip shipments reached 98 billion units, a year-on-year increase of 35%. Among them, the domestic substitution rate for mature process chips has risen from about 15% in 2020 to over 40% currently. Multiple domestic chip design companies have achieved notable breakthroughs in niche markets such as the Internet of Things, power management, and RF chips. Industry insiders indicate that although advanced process technologies still face challenges, China's chip industry has developed strong competitiveness in the mature process segment.

The rapid development of China's chip industry also benefits from the pull of a vast domestic market. Demand for chips in new energy vehicles, industrial control, smart homes, and other fields remains robust, providing domestic chip companies with a broad platform for application validation. Continuous investment from the National Integrated Circuit Industry Investment Fund has also provided financial support for corporate R&D. At the same time, Chinese chip companies are actively participating in the formulation of international standards, enhancing their voice and influence in the global semiconductor industry.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that related changes will have a profound impact on the development landscape of the sector. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to usher in faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are full of expectations for this.
