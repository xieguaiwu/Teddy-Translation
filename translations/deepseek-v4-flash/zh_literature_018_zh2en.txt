Parting the sorghum, Father scurried swiftly toward the northwest, toward our village. A badger stumbled clumsily along the furrows, but Father paid it no mind. "Wuchengzi"  
Now lost. The Wuxing school of thought recorded "Wuchengzi's Disasters and Responses" in fourteen volumes, and the Fangzhong school recorded "Wuchengzi's Yin Dao" in thirty-six volumes, both now lost. Wucheng is a compound surname; his given name was Zhao, or according to some, Fu. Wang Fu of the Eastern Han Dynasty, in his "Qianfu Lun: Praise of Learning," recorded that "Yao took Wucheng as his teacher."
