Zhu Yan's injury healed slowly, and one afternoon, Grocer Zhang finally secured some benefits for him: the school paid part of the cost for treating his leg, and the person in charge of logistics even bought a few catties of beef bones for him. Grocer Zhang brought them home in high spirits, boiled the beef bones, and before he could ladle out a bowl of the fragrant, buttery bone broth for Zhu Yan, he first filled a bowl for himself and drank it down greedily. As he drank, he thought: he must eat the entire pot of bones before Zhu Mei came home, leaving not a shred of meat or a drop of soup for her. In the past, she had grudged the old lady's eating; now she strongly encouraged it. So by the time Zhu Mei returned home, although the grocery store was filled with an enticing meaty aroma, there was nothing left in the pot. Zhu Mei squinted at the bare, gleaming white bones piled by the stove, gritted her teeth and cursed: "You three white-eyed wolves, you three bastards!"
