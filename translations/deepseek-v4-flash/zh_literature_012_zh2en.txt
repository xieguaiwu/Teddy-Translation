That very afternoon, the conference presidium announced group discussions, and the waitstaff delivered the three newspapers subscribed for the representatives during the conference. Those who had already spoken continued to speak, while those who hadn't opened their newspapers. Zhuang Zhidie first read the literary section on the third page of the provincial newspaper, then looked at the city newspaper. Almost the entire first two pages were filled with various reports about the conference, which he found uninteresting, so he turned to the third newspaper called *Weekend* and was immediately attracted by a piece of news. The headline read: 'City Government Office Slack in Starting Work; Over Half Arrive After Half an Hour.' The content was about how a reporter from this newspaper suddenly conducted a survey at the city government gate on a certain day at work time: how many people arrived ten minutes after work started, how many after twenty minutes, how many after half an hour. How many bureau chiefs were late, how many deputy mayors were late. Instantly, the meeting buzzed with discussion, and the topic shifted from debating the mayor's government work report to arguing about this news report. Zhuang Zhidie listened, finding it nothing but a chaotic chorus of complaints, and felt bored. He returned to his room and called home to ask if there was anything urgent. The person who answered was Liu Yue, who kept asking, 'Who? Who?' Just as Zhuang Zhidie was about to speak, he heard laughter and horseplay on the line. He wanted to hear who was playing around, so he didn't say anything. Liu Yue on the other end said, 'Crazy!' and hung up with a click. Zhuang Zhidie dialed again. Liu Yue, without asking what was what, shouted, 'Wrong number, this is the crematorium!' and hung up again. Furious, Zhuang Zhidie called once more. As soon as someone picked up, he scolded, 'Liu Yue, is this how you answer the phone at home?!' Liu Yue recognized his voice and quickly said, 'Teacher Zhuang, how is it you? You haven't been around these days. Dozens of calls come for you every day. I said you weren't in, and then the calls would come again. Big Sister told me to say the number was wrong. I never thought I'd mess up your call.' Zhuang Zhidie was still angry: 'Who was talking to you there?' Liu Yue said, 'It was Hong Jiang. He just came looking for you. Do you want to talk to him?' At first, he muttered indistinctly, then got to the matter of the bookstore and immediately said that a certain manuscript had been printed two days ago and distributed to retail outlets everywhere, selling very well. Hong Jiang babbled on for a while, and Zhuang Zhidie didn't make a sound. Hong Jiang said, 'Teacher Zhuang, are you listening?' Zhuang Zhidie said, 'Hmm.' Hong Jiang said, 'This time we've really hit the jackpot. I roughly calculated, we invested a hundred thousand, and we can net thirty thousand!
