China's Digital Economy Scale Exceeds 60 Trillion Yuan

The "Digital China Development Report" released by the Cyberspace Administration of China shows that in 2025, the scale of China's digital economy exceeded 60 trillion yuan, ranking second globally for eight consecutive years. The report points out that the deep integration of digital technology and the real economy is reshaping the traditional industrial landscape. Among them, the application coverage rate of industrial internet platforms has reached 78%, and the average production efficiency of smart manufacturing demonstration projects has increased by 32%. The market-oriented allocation reform of data factors is also steadily advancing, with the annual transaction volume of data exchanges in Beijing, Shanghai, Guangdong and other places exceeding 50 billion yuan in total.

Industry analysts point out that the rapid development of the digital economy is not only reflected in scale growth, but also in structural optimization. The proportion of industrial digitization in the digital economy has exceeded 80%, and the digital transformation of traditional manufacturing is accelerating. The deep integration of new-generation information technologies such as 5G, artificial intelligence, and big data with manufacturing has given rise to new models and formats such as smart factories, personalized customization, and collaborative manufacturing. The construction of digital villages is also being promoted comprehensively, with application scenarios such as rural e-commerce and smart agriculture continuously expanding, and the urban-rural digital divide gradually narrowing. The legal and regulatory system for data security and personal information protection is also being continuously improved.
