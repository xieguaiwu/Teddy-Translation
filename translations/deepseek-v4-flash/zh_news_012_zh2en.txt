Major Breakthrough in Deep-Sea Exploration Technology

The China Ocean Mineral Resources R&D Association announced that the Fendouzhe (Striver) manned submersible has completed its 100th dive mission in the Mariana Trench in the Pacific Ocean, reaching a maximum depth of 10,909 meters. Meanwhile, domestically developed deep-sea equipment, such as deep-sea drilling vessels and seafloor observation networks, are progressing steadily. China's technological reserves for the exploration and development of deep-sea mineral resources have entered the forefront of the world. Scientists state that deep-sea research holds irreplaceable scientific value for understanding Earth's evolution, climate change, and the origin of life.

Advances in deep-sea exploration technology have also provided new means for marine resource development and marine environmental protection. Deep-sea mineral resources mainly include polymetallic nodules, cobalt-rich crusts, and hydrothermal sulfides, which contain strategic metal elements such as nickel, cobalt, copper, and manganese. China holds several exclusive exploration contract areas in the international seabed region. Research on deep-sea ecological environments is progressing simultaneously, with scientists using deep-sea detectors to conduct long-term observations of special ecosystems such as hydrothermal vents and cold seeps, discovering a large number of new species.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that relevant changes will have a profound impact on the development landscape of the industry. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a beneficial interaction. In the coming years, with the deepening of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to usher in faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are full of expectations for this.
