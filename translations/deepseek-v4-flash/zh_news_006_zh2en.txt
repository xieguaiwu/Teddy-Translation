China Space Station Welcomes Fifth Batch of Scientific Experiments

The China Manned Space Agency announced that the Tiangong Space Station has officially launched its fifth batch of scientific experiments. This batch includes 32 projects covering fields such as space life sciences, microgravity physics, space materials science, and astronomical observations, with international cooperation projects accounting for 40%. Research teams from 17 countries will conduct experiments aboard the China Space Station. To date, the Tiangong Space Station has completed over 200 scientific experiments, yielding a number of research results with international influence.

The international cooperation model of the China Space Station has received positive evaluations from the United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs. Through various forms such as joint experiments, astronaut exchanges, and data sharing, the China Space Station is becoming a new platform for global space research collaboration. The station's scalable design also provides a technology verification platform for future deep-space exploration missions. Related technological research and development for crewed lunar missions are also progressing, with the goal of achieving a Chinese astronaut lunar landing within this decade.
