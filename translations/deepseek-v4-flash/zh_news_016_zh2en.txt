The total number of 5G base stations has exceeded 5 million.

Data released by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology shows that as of April 2026, the total number of 5G base stations in China has reached 5.12 million, covering all prefecture-level cities, county urban areas, and the vast majority of townships. The number of 5G users has surpassed 1.2 billion, with a penetration rate of 68%. The application of 5G in fields such as industrial internet, smart mining, and telemedicine continues to deepen, with 5G directly driving an estimated total economic output of approximately 3.8 trillion yuan. Research and development of 6G has also entered the phase of tackling key technologies.

The integrated application of 5G technology is giving rise to new business models and industrial forms. In the industrial sector, the use of 5G private networks has made scenarios such as remote equipment control, machine vision quality inspection, and unmanned inspection a reality. In the medical field, 5G remote surgery and mobile diagnosis and treatment have improved the accessibility of high-quality medical resources. In the cultural sector, 5G+8K ultra-high-definition video live streaming and cloud performances have enriched people's cultural lives. The research focus of 6G includes cutting-edge technologies such as terahertz communication, intelligent reconfigurable surfaces, and space-air-ground integrated networks.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that the related changes will have a profound impact on the development landscape of the industry. In the long term, all parties need to strengthen coordination and collaboration to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, with the in-depth advancement of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to usher in faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are full of expectations for this.
