National Railway Single-Day Passenger Volume Hits Record High

According to China State Railway Group Co., Ltd., on the first day of the May Day holiday, the national railway network carried 21.24 million passengers, setting a new single-day record for passenger volume. The railway department added 1,200 passenger trains that day to fully meet travel demand. The share of high-speed rail travel continues to rise, with an average of over 10,000 high-speed trains operating daily during the holiday period. The railway 12306 system maintained stable operation during peak times, with daily ticket sales exceeding 20 million at their highest.

The quality of railway passenger services is also continuously improving. Electronic tickets are now fully adopted, and convenient services such as face-scanning entry and smart navigation are available at more stations. High-speed rail dining has introduced local specialty foods and online ordering services. Humanized services such as quiet carriages, nursing rooms, and accessible facilities are being steadily enhanced. The railway department is also advancing the development of metropolitan and intercity railways, promoting the integration of trunk railways, intercity railways, suburban railways, and urban rail transit—a four-network integration.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that the relevant changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and collaboration to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation deepen, related fields are expected to usher in faster and higher-quality development. Society at large is filled with anticipation for this.
