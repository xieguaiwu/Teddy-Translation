Domestic large aircraft C919 adds new international routes

COMAC announced today that the domestically produced C919 large aircraft will add three new international routes starting next month, including Shanghai to Singapore, Beijing to Bangkok, and Guangzhou to Kuala Lumpur. To date, 28 C919 aircraft have been delivered, with over 100,000 hours of safe flight and a load factor consistently above 85%. The type certification review by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) has entered its final stage and is expected to be completed within the year. This marks a key step forward for China's civil aviation manufacturing industry in its internationalization process.

COMAC stated that the C919's internationalization strategy extends beyond route expansion to include overseas maintenance network construction and pilot training cooperation. Several international aircraft leasing companies have expressed procurement intentions for the C919. Industry experts believe that the C919's entry into the international market will reshape the global narrow-body aircraft market, which has long been dominated by the Boeing 737 and Airbus A320. The Civil Aviation Administration of China has also stated that it will further strengthen cooperation with aviation safety regulatory agencies in Europe and the United States to promote the safe operation of domestically produced aircraft worldwide.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that related changes will have a profound impact on the development landscape of the sector. In the long run, all parties need to enhance coordination and collaboration to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to see faster and higher-quality development. There is great anticipation from all sectors of society.
