New Curriculum Standards for Compulsory Education Fully Implemented

The Ministry of Education announced that starting from the fall semester of 2026, the new curriculum standards for compulsory education will be fully implemented in primary and secondary schools nationwide. The new standards focus on a core competency-oriented approach, strengthening the proportion of science, labor, and arts education. Specifically, the starting grade for science courses has been adjusted from third grade to first grade, while the share of class hours for physical education and health has been increased to 10% to 11%. Textbook compilation and teacher training have been fully launched, expected to cover approximately 160 million primary and secondary school students.

The advancement of compulsory education reform also faces challenges such as balanced regional development and the building of teaching faculties. The operating conditions of schools in the central and western regions and rural areas are being continuously improved, and policies such as the Special Post Program and the Silver-Age Lecture Program have been used to replenish teachers in shortage subjects. The educational digitalization strategy has promoted the co-construction and sharing of high-quality educational resources. The continued implementation of the "Double Reduction" policy has alleviated students' academic burden and off-campus training burden. Education evaluation reform is also exploring the establishment of a more scientific and diversified evaluation system.

This trend has triggered widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen synergy and coordination to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, with the further advancement of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to usher in more rapid and high-quality development. All sectors of society are looking forward to this with great anticipation.
