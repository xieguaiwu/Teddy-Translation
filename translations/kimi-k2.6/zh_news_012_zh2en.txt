Major Progress Achieved in Deep-Sea Exploration Technology

The China Ocean Mineral Resources Research and Development Association (COMRA) announced that the Fendouzhe manned submersible has completed its 100th dive mission in the Mariana Trench of the Pacific Ocean, reaching a maximum depth of 10,909 meters. Meanwhile, China's independently developed deep-sea equipment—including deep-sea drilling vessels and seafloor observation networks—is also making steady progress. The nation's technological reserves for the exploration and development of deep-sea mineral resources now rank among the world's best. Scientists say deep-sea research holds irreplaceable scientific value for understanding Earth's evolution, climate change, and the origin of life.

Advances in deep-sea exploration technology have also provided new means for marine resource development and marine environmental protection. Deep-sea mineral resources mainly include polymetallic nodules, cobalt-rich crusts, and hydrothermal sulfides, containing strategic metal elements such as nickel, cobalt, copper, and manganese. China holds multiple exclusive exploration contract areas in the international seabed zone. Research on deep-sea ecological environments is also advancing in parallel; using deep-sea detectors, scientists have conducted long-term observations of special ecosystems such as hydrothermal vents and cold seeps, discovering a large number of new species.

This trend has triggered widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. From a long-term perspective, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. Continuous policy optimization and positive market feedback have formed a benign interaction. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation advance further, related fields are expected to usher in faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are eagerly anticipating this.
