China's Railways Set Record for Single-Day Passenger Volume

According to China State Railway Group, the national railway system handled 21.24 million passenger trips on the first day of the May Day holiday, setting a new all-time high for single-day passenger volume. Railway authorities added 1,200 passenger trains that day to fully meet travel demand. The share of high-speed rail travel continues to rise, with more than 10,000 EMU trains operated daily during the holiday period. The 12306 railway ticketing system maintained stable operation during peak times, with daily ticket sales exceeding 20 million at their peak.

The quality of railway passenger services is also improving continuously. E-tickets have been fully implemented, and convenient services such as facial-recognition station entry and smart navigation now cover more stations. High-speed rail dining has introduced local specialty cuisines and online meal-ordering services. Human-centered services such as quiet carriages, nursing rooms, and barrier-free facilities are being continuously improved. Railway authorities are also advancing the development of suburban and intercity railways, promoting the integration of the four networks: trunk railways, intercity railways, suburban railways, and urban rail transit.

This trend has attracted widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly meet challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation advance further, related fields are expected to see faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are eagerly anticipating this.
