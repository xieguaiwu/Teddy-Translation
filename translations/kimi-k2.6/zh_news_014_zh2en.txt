World's Largest Offshore Wind Power Project Connected to Grid

The world's largest offshore wind power project, built by a Chinese company, has achieved full-capacity grid connection in the waters off Yangjiang, Guangdong. The project has a total installed capacity of 6.5 GW and an annual power generation of approximately 26 billion kWh, enough to meet the yearly electricity demand of about 9 million households. It utilizes multiple models of domestically produced large-capacity wind turbines, reducing construction costs. As an important component of clean energy, offshore wind power is becoming a key pillar of China's energy transition.

The construction of offshore wind power projects has also driven exports of related equipment manufacturing and engineering and technical services. Chinese-made wind turbines and installation vessels have been exported to Europe, Southeast Asia, and other countries and regions. Integrated development models that combine offshore wind power with marine ranching, seawater hydrogen production, and wave energy generation are also being explored. Breakthroughs in deep-sea floating wind power technology have laid the foundation for developing wind energy resources in deeper and more distant waters. The rapid development of the offshore wind power industry provides important support for China to achieve its carbon peaking and carbon neutrality goals.

This trend has triggered widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. From a long-term perspective, all parties need to strengthen synergy and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. Continuous policy optimization and positive market feedback have formed a positive interaction. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation advance further, related fields are expected to embrace more rapid and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are eagerly looking forward to this.
