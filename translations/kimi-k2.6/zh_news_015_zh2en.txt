China's Grain Output Remains Above 1.3 Trillion Jin for Eight Consecutive Years

Data released by the National Bureau of Statistics on 2025 grain output shows that total national grain production reached 1.3982 trillion jin, marking the eighth consecutive year above 1.3 trillion jin. Both soybean planting area and output achieved dual growth, with notable results from expansion efforts. The contribution rate of agricultural science and technology progress rose to 65%, and the comprehensive mechanization rate of crop cultivation, planting, and harvesting reached 74%. Cumulative high-standard farmland construction has reached 1 billion mu, further solidifying the foundation of food security.

The foundation of food security lies in farmland protection and seed industry innovation. National arable land area remains stable above 1.865 billion mu, while high-standard farmland construction continuously improves land quality. The seed industry revitalization initiative is being thoroughly implemented, and the planted area of independently developed superior varieties is expanding year by year. Agricultural green transformation is also accelerating, with chemical fertilizer and pesticide use achieving negative growth for multiple consecutive years. The application of smart agricultural technology has improved water resource utilization efficiency and the precision of pest and disease control. Grain storage and logistics systems are continuously improving, reducing post-harvest losses.

This trend has drawn widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts note that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry development landscape. From a long-term perspective, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. Continuous policy optimization and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation are further advanced, relevant sectors are expected to see more rapid and high-quality development. All sectors of society eagerly await this.
