Zhuang Zhidie first read the literary section on page three of the provincial newspaper, then looked at the city newspaper—almost the first two pages were entirely various reports about the conference—and felt it was rather meaningless, so he went to read the third newspaper called *Weekend*, and was immediately attracted by a piece of news. The headline was: "City Government Compound Sluggish at Work, Over Half Arrive After Half an Hour." The content was actually that the newspaper's reporter had suddenly conducted a survey at the city government gate on such-and-such date when arriving at work: how many people came ten minutes after work started, how many came twenty minutes after, how many came half an hour after. How many bureau chiefs were late, how many deputy mayors were late. Immediately the meeting was abuzz with discussion, and the topic shifted from discussing the mayor's government work report to arguing about this news report. Zhuang Zhidie listened—it was nothing but noisy complaining—and felt it was completely boring, so he returned to his room and called home to ask if there was anything urgent. The one who answered was Liu Yue, asking straight away, "Who is it? Who is it?" Zhuang Zhidie was about to speak when the sound of horseplay came through the phone.
