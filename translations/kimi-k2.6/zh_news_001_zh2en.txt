China's Digital Economy Exceeds 60 Trillion Yuan

The Digital China Development Report recently released by the Cyberspace Administration of China shows that in 2025, China's digital economy exceeded 60 trillion yuan, ranking second globally for eight consecutive years. The report points out that the deep integration of digital technology and the real economy is reshaping the traditional industrial landscape. Among these developments, the application coverage rate of industrial internet platforms has reached 78%, and production efficiency at smart manufacturing demonstration projects has increased by an average of 32%. Reform toward market-oriented allocation of data elements is also advancing steadily, with combined annual trading volume at data exchanges in Beijing, Shanghai, Guangdong, and other locations exceeding 50 billion yuan.

Industry insiders note that the rapid development of the digital economy is reflected not only in scale growth, but even more so in structural optimization. Industrial digitalization now accounts for more than 80% of the digital economy, and the digital transformation of traditional manufacturing is accelerating. The deep integration of new-generation information technologies such as 5G, artificial intelligence, and big data with manufacturing has given rise to new models and business formats such as smart factories, personalized customization, and collaborative manufacturing. Digital rural construction is also being comprehensively advanced, with application scenarios such as rural e-commerce and smart agriculture continuously expanding, gradually narrowing the urban-rural digital divide. The legal and regulatory framework for data security and personal information protection is also being continuously improved.
