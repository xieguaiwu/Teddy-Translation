Thinking about really chopping off her arm, she still couldn't bring herself to do it. But from then on, she became even more overbearing and rude to the old lady. Zhu Yan's injury healed slowly. Grocer Zhang finally won some benefits for Zhu Yan one afternoon: the school paid part of the money for treating his leg, and the person in charge of logistics even bought him several pounds of beef bones. Grocer Zhang enthusiastically carried them home, stewed the beef bones, and before serving Zhu Yan a bowl of fragrant beef bone soup with yellow oil flowing, she first served herself a bowl and drank it with gusto. While drinking, she thought that she should take advantage of the time before Zhu Mei returned home to gnaw all the bones in the pot, leaving her not a shred of meat or a drop of soup. Previously she had felt heartache when the old lady ate things, but this time she greatly encouraged her, so that by the time Zhu Mei came home, although the grocery shop was permeated with an alluring meat aroma, there was nothing left in the pot. Zhu Mei squinted at the bones by the stove, picked clean and gleaming white, and gnashed her teeth cursing: "You three white-eyed wolves, you three turtle-grandsons!" None of the three had the strength to quarrel with her; they had simply eaten too much and were too tired. Zhu Mei grabbed a bone and threw it at the mirror on the wall.
