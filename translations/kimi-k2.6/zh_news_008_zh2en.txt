China's High-Speed Railway Mileage Exceeds 55,000 Kilometers

Data released by China State Railway Group shows that China's high-speed railway operating mileage has exceeded 55,000 kilometers, accounting for more than two-thirds of the global total. Approximately 2,500 kilometers of new high-speed lines are planned for 2026, including major projects such as the Chongqing-Kunming and Xi'an-Chengdu high-speed railways. The continued expansion of the high-speed rail network is profoundly reshaping the regional economic landscape, significantly reducing travel times between urban agglomerations such as Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei, the Yangtze River Delta, and the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area. High-speed rail passengers now account for 75% of total railway passenger volume.

The economic impact of the high-speed rail network is radiating from the transportation sector into broader regional economies. Development of new urban districts, industrial parks, and commercial facilities around high-speed rail stations continues to improve, forming what are known as high-speed rail economic belts. The tourism industry has been a particularly notable beneficiary, with tourism revenue in small and medium-sized cities along high-speed rail lines increasing significantly. High-speed rail freight services are also being explored to provide rapid transport for high-value-added goods. Research and development of 400 km/h high-speed maglev trains has also entered the testing and verification phase.

This trend has attracted widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts note that these changes will have a far-reaching impact on the development landscape of the sector. In the long term, all parties need to strengthen coordination to jointly meet challenges and seize opportunities. Continuous policy optimization and positive market feedback have created a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation advance further, related fields are expected to achieve faster and higher-quality development. Society is eagerly anticipating this.
