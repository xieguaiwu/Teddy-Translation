Significant Growth in Shipments of China’s Self-Developed Chips

Data released by the China Semiconductor Industry Association shows that domestic chip shipments reached 98 billion units in the first quarter of 2026, a year-on-year increase of 35%. Among these, the domestic substitution rate for mature-process chips has risen from approximately 15% in 2020 to over 40% at present. Multiple domestic chip design companies have achieved notable breakthroughs in niche markets such as the Internet of Things, power management, and radio frequency chips. Industry insiders stated that despite ongoing challenges in advanced processes, China’s chip industry has developed strong competitiveness in the mature-process sector.

The rapid development of China’s chip industry is also driven by the pull of a massive domestic demand market. Sustained strong chip demand in areas such as new energy vehicles, industrial control, and smart homes has provided domestic chip companies with broad application verification platforms. Continuous investment from the National Integrated Circuit Industry Investment Fund has also provided financial support for corporate research and development. Meanwhile, Chinese chip companies are actively participating in the formulation of international standards, enhancing their voice and influence in the global semiconductor industry.

This trend has triggered widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have far-reaching effects on the industry development landscape. From a long-term perspective, all parties need to strengthen synergy and coordination to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation advance further, related fields are expected to embrace more rapid and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are eagerly anticipating this.
