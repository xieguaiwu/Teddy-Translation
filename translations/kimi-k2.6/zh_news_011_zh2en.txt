China's Forest Coverage Rises to 25.3%

Latest data released by the National Forestry and Grassland Administration shows that China's national forest coverage has increased to 25.3%, with forest area reaching 245 million hectares. Over the past decade, China has added more than 60 million hectares of forest area, making it the country with the largest forest resource growth globally. The Three-North Shelterbelt Program, the Grain for Green Project, and national land greening initiatives continue to advance. International environmental organizations have noted that China's afforestation efforts have made substantive contributions to global carbon reduction and biodiversity conservation.

The service function value of forest ecosystems is also continuously increasing. The forest carbon sink trading market is gradually improving, and forestry carbon sink projects have provided new income channels for forest area residents. Pilot programs for the national park system are being advanced in depth, and a nature reserve system with national parks as the mainstay is taking shape. A comprehensive system for natural forest protection and restoration has been established, with responsibility for forest resource management assigned to specific individuals. Green industries such as under-forest economy, forest tourism, and forest wellness are flourishing, achieving a win-win outcome for ecological protection and economic development.

This trend has triggered widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation advance in depth, related fields are expected to embrace faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are looking forward to this with great anticipation.
