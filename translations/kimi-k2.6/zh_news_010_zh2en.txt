Digital RMB Pilot Scope Expanded Nationwide

The People's Bank of China announced that the digital RMB pilot program will be expanded from the current 26 cities to nationwide coverage. As of May 2026, cumulative digital RMB transaction volume has exceeded 2.5 trillion yuan, and the number of individual wallets opened has surpassed 380 million. Cross-border payment pilots are also being advanced, with interoperability mechanisms established with multiple countries and regions. The Digital Currency Research Institute of the central bank stated that the promotion and application of digital RMB will further enhance the efficiency and security of the payment system.

The promotion of digital RMB is also gradually changing residents' payment habits. Compared with third-party payment platforms, digital RMB offers unique advantages such as controllable anonymity, dual offline payment, and programmability. In scenarios such as government subsidy distribution, cross-border trade settlement, and supply chain finance, the application of digital RMB is being piloted and advanced. The central bank stated that digital RMB is positioned as a digital replacement for cash, which will not change the monetary policy transmission mechanism, but will provide more tools for precise regulation.

This trend has triggered widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry development landscape. From a long-term perspective, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. Continuous policy optimization and positive market feedback have formed a positive interaction. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation advance in depth, related fields are expected to see faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are eagerly anticipating this.
