China's Cumulative NEV Ownership Exceeds 40 Million Units

The latest data from the Ministry of Public Security shows that as of the end of May 2026, the national new energy vehicle (NEV) fleet has reached 41.5 million units, accounting for 12.7% of the total automobile stock. In the first five months of 2026, newly registered NEVs hit 4.2 million units, up 45% year-on-year. Regarding charging infrastructure, the total number of charging piles nationwide has reached 12 million units, with the vehicle-to-pile ratio improving to 3.5:1. Industry insiders believe that with advances in battery technology and improvements to the charging network, NEV market penetration will exceed 30% by 2027.

The rapid development of the NEV industry has also driven coordinated progress across upstream and downstream industrial chains. The energy density of power batteries continues to improve, while costs for lithium iron phosphate and ternary material batteries have dropped by approximately 60% and 50%, respectively. Next-generation battery technologies such as solid-state and sodium-ion batteries are also being accelerated in R&D. The integrated application of intelligent connected vehicle technology has significantly enhanced user experience in areas such as autonomous driving and smart cockpits. The layout of charging and battery swap infrastructure is expanding to highway service areas, townships, and residential communities.

This trend has triggered widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts note that these changes will exert a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation advance in depth, relevant sectors are expected to embrace faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are looking forward to this with great anticipation.
