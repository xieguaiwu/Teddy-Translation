Urban Renewal Initiative Drives Renovation of Old Residential Communities

The Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development's latest bulletin shows that renovation work has commenced on a cumulative total of 280,000 old urban residential communities nationwide, benefiting more than 65 million households. The 2026 plan targets the renovation of 53,000 old residential communities, with a focus on installing elevators, upgrading water, electricity, gas, and heating pipelines, and adding parking spaces and electric vehicle charging stations. Local governments are also exploring new models to introduce private capital into renovation and operation. Experts point out that urban renewal is not only a livelihood project, but also an important means of stimulating domestic demand and promoting economic growth.

In the process of renovating old residential communities, local governments are also exploring the establishment of long-term management mechanisms. Through the introduction of professional property management, the establishment of homeowners' committees, and the creation of maintenance funds, renovated communities are ensuring the sustainability of these improvements. The integration of smart community construction with old community renovation has promoted the adoption of smart access control, intelligent parking, and smart waste sorting facilities. Resident satisfaction surveys show that the average living comfort in renovated communities has improved by more than 40%.

This trend has triggered widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts note that these changes will have a profound impact on the sector's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation advance further, related fields are expected to witness faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are looking forward to this with great anticipation.
