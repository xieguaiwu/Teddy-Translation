Total 5G Base Stations Exceed 5 Million

Data released by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology shows that as of April 2026, China's total number of 5G base stations has reached 5.12 million, covering all prefecture-level cities, county seats, and the vast majority of townships. The number of 5G subscribers has surpassed 1.2 billion, with a penetration rate of 68%. The application of 5G in industrial internet, smart mining, remote healthcare, and other fields continues to deepen, with 5G directly driving total economic output of approximately 3.8 trillion yuan. 6G research and development has also entered a critical stage of tackling key technologies.

The integrated application of 5G technology is giving rise to new business models and industrial forms. In the industrial sector, the application of 5G private networks has made scenarios such as remote equipment control, machine vision quality inspection, and unmanned patrols a reality. In the healthcare sector, 5G remote surgery and mobile diagnosis and treatment have improved the accessibility of quality medical resources. In the cultural sector, 5G+8K ultra-high-definition video livestreaming and cloud-based performances have enriched people's cultural lives. 6G R&D focuses on frontier technologies including terahertz communications, intelligent reflecting surfaces, and integrated space-air-ground networks.

This trend has drawn widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. Continuous policy optimization and positive market feedback have formed a positive interaction. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation advance in depth, relevant fields are expected to embrace more rapid and high-quality development. All sectors of society are looking forward to this.
