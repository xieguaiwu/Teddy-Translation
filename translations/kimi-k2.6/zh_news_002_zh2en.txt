China's Homegrown C919 Adds International Routes

COMAC announced today that the domestically produced C919 jet will add three international routes starting next month: Shanghai to Singapore, Beijing to Bangkok, and Guangzhou to Kuala Lumpur. To date, the C919 has seen 28 cumulative deliveries, with safe flight operations exceeding 100,000 hours and a passenger load factor maintained above 85%. The type certification review by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) has also entered its final stage and is expected to be completed within the year. This marks a crucial step for China's civil aviation manufacturing industry in its internationalization process.

COMAC stated that the C919's internationalization strategy extends beyond route expansion to include establishing overseas maintenance networks and cooperating on pilot training. Several international aircraft leasing companies have expressed purchase intentions for the C919. Industry observers believe the aircraft's entry into the international market will alter the long-standing duopoly of Boeing's 737 and Airbus's A320 in the global narrow-body market. The Civil Aviation Administration of China (CAAC) also said it will further strengthen cooperation with European and American aviation safety regulators to promote safe worldwide operations of domestically produced civil aircraft.

This trend has drawn widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts note that these changes will exert a far-reaching influence on the sector's development landscape. In the long term, all parties need to enhance synergy to jointly meet challenges and seize opportunities. Continuous policy optimization and positive market feedback have created a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation advance further, related fields are expected to achieve more rapid and higher-quality development. Society at large looks forward to this with great anticipation.
