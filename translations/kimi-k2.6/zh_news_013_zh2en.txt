China's Sports Industry Total Scale Exceeds 5 Trillion Yuan

Data released by the General Administration of Sport of China shows that in 2025, the total scale of China's sports industry reached 5.2 trillion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 15.8%. The national fitness movement continues to gain momentum, with the proportion of people regularly participating in physical exercise reaching 38.5%. More than 800 marathon events were held throughout the year, with over 10 million participants. The sports consumption structure is also being optimized, with emerging business formats such as sports training, sports tourism, and smart sports equipment growing rapidly.

The digital transformation of the sports industry is also accelerating. New business formats such as smart sports equipment, online fitness platforms, and e-sports are developing rapidly. Traditional businesses including sports event broadcasting rights, sports IP operations, and sports lottery are also maintaining steady growth. Since national fitness was elevated to a national strategy, local governments have increased investment in sports infrastructure, with community sports parks, fitness trails, and multi-purpose sports venues continuing to multiply. Cross-sector collaboration models such as the integration of sports and education and the integration of sports and medicine are also being explored.

This trend has triggered widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. Continuous policy optimization and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, as technological iteration and model innovation advance in depth, related fields are expected to usher in faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are looking forward to this.
