China's Digital Economy Surpasses 60 Trillion Yuan

The "Digital China Development Report" recently released by the Cyberspace Administration of China shows that the scale of China's digital economy exceeded 60 trillion yuan in 2025, ranking second globally for the eighth consecutive year. The report points out that the deep integration of digital technology and the real economy is reshaping the landscape of traditional industries. Specifically, the application coverage rate of industrial internet platforms has reached 78%, and the production efficiency of intelligent manufacturing demonstration projects has increased by an average of 32%. Reforms on the market-oriented allocation of data factors are also advancing steadily, with the combined annual transaction volume of data exchanges in Beijing, Shanghai, Guangdong, and other regions exceeding 50 billion yuan.

Industry insiders point out that the rapid development of the digital economy is reflected not only in scale growth but also in structural optimization. Industrial digitalization now accounts for over 80% of the digital economy, and the digital transformation of traditional manufacturing is accelerating. The deep integration of next-generation information technologies, such as 5G, artificial intelligence, and big data, with the manufacturing sector has spawned new models and business formats, including smart factories, personalized customization, and collaborative manufacturing. The construction of digital villages is also being comprehensively advanced, with application scenarios such as rural e-commerce and smart agriculture continuously expanding, gradually narrowing the urban-rural digital divide. The legal and regulatory framework for data security and personal information protection is also being continuously improved.
