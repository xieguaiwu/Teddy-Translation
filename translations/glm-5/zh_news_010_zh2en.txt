Digital RMB Pilot Program Expanded Nationwide

The People's Bank of China announced that the digital RMB pilot will be expanded nationwide from the current 26 cities. As of May 2026, the cumulative transaction volume of the digital RMB has exceeded 2.5 trillion yuan, and the number of opened personal wallets has surpassed 380 million. Cross-border payment pilots are also advancing, with interoperability mechanisms established with multiple countries and regions. The central bank's Digital Currency Research Institute stated that the promotion and application of the digital RMB will further enhance the efficiency and security of the payment system.

The promotion of the digital RMB is also gradually changing residents' payment habits. Compared to third-party payment platforms, the digital RMB boasts unique advantages such as controllable anonymity, dual offline payment, and programmability. The application of the digital RMB is currently being piloted in scenarios such as the distribution of government subsidies, cross-border trade settlement, and supply chain finance. The central bank stated that the digital RMB is positioned as a digital replacement for cash and will not alter the transmission mechanism of monetary policy, but will provide more tool options for precise regulation.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape.
