Total Scale of China's Sports Industry Surpasses 5 Trillion Yuan

Data released by the General Administration of Sport of China shows that in 2025, the total scale of China's sports industry reached 5.2 trillion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 15.8%. The national fitness movement continues to gain momentum, with the proportion of people regularly participating in physical exercise reaching 38.5%. Over 800 marathon events were held throughout the year, with total attendance exceeding 10 million. The structure of sports consumption is also being optimized, with emerging sectors such as sports training, sports tourism, and smart sports equipment growing rapidly.

The digital transformation of the sports industry is also accelerating. New business models such as smart sports equipment, online fitness platforms, and e-sports are developing rapidly. Traditional businesses, including sports event broadcasting rights, sports IP operations, and sports lotteries, are also maintaining steady growth. Since national fitness was elevated to a national strategy, local governments have increased investment in sports infrastructure, leading to a continuous increase in facilities such as community sports parks, fitness trails, and multi-functional sports fields. Cross-sector cooperation models, such as the integration of sports and education and the integration of sports and healthcare, are also being explored.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, as technological iteration and business model innovation continue to deepen, related fields are expected to experience more rapid and high-quality development. All sectors of society hold high expectations for this.
