Total Number of 5G Base Stations Exceeds 5 Million

Data released by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology shows that as of April 2026, the total number of 5G base stations in China has reached 5.12 million, covering all prefecture-level cities, county seats, and the vast majority of townships nationwide. The number of 5G users has exceeded 1.2 billion, with a penetration rate of 68%. The application of 5G in fields such as the industrial internet, smart mining, and telemedicine continues to deepen, and 5G has directly driven a total economic output of approximately 3.8 trillion yuan. 6G research and development has also entered the stage of tackling key technologies.

The integrated application of 5G technology is giving rise to new business models and industrial forms. In the industrial sector, the application of 5G private networks has made scenarios such as remote equipment control, machine vision quality inspection, and unmanned inspection a reality. In the medical field, 5G remote surgery and mobile diagnosis and treatment have improved the accessibility of high-quality medical resources. In the cultural sector, 5G+8K ultra-high-definition video live broadcasts and cloud performances have enriched people's cultural lives. 6G R&D focuses on cutting-edge technologies such as terahertz communication, reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, and space-air-ground integrated networks.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the landscape of industry development. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening advancement of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to experience more rapid and high-quality development. All sectors of society hold high expectations for this.
