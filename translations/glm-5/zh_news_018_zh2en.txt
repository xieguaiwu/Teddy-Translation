National Railway Single-Day Passenger Volume Hits Record

According to China State Railway Group Co., Ltd., national railways transported 21.24 million passengers on the first day of the May Day holiday, setting a new record for single-day passenger volume. The railway department added 1,200 passenger trains that day to fully meet passenger travel demand. The proportion of high-speed rail travel continued to rise, with an average of over 10,000 EMU trains operating daily during the holiday. The 12306 railway ticketing system maintained smooth operation during peak hours, with daily ticket sales peaking at over 20 million.

The quality of railway passenger services is also continuously improving. E-tickets have been fully implemented, and convenient services such as facial recognition station entry and smart navigation are now available at more stations. High-speed rail catering has introduced local specialty foods and online ordering services. Passenger-friendly services like quiet carriages, nursing rooms, and accessible facilities are continuously being improved. The railway department is also advancing the development of municipal and intercity railways, promoting the four-network integration of trunk railways, intercity railways, suburban railways, and urban rail transit.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous interaction. In the coming years, with the deep advancement of technological iterations and model innovations, related fields are expected to experience more rapid and high-quality development. All sectors of society are full of expectations for this.
