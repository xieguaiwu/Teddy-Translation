China's High-Speed Rail Mileage Exceeds 55,000 Kilometers

Data released by China State Railway Group shows that China's high-speed rail operating mileage has exceeded 55,000 kilometers, accounting for over two-thirds of the world's total. Approximately 2,500 kilometers of new high-speed rail lines are planned for 2026, including major projects like the Chongqing-Kunming and Xi'an-Chengdu high-speed railways. The continuous expansion of the high-speed rail network is profoundly reshaping the regional economic landscape, significantly reducing commuting times between major city clusters such as the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region, the Yangtze River Delta, and the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area. The proportion of high-speed rail passengers to total railway passenger traffic has risen to 75%.

The economic impact of the high-speed rail network is extending beyond the transportation sector to permeate the broader regional economy. The development of new urban districts, the construction of industrial parks, and the improvement of commercial facilities around high-speed rail stations are continuously advancing, forming what is known as the high-speed rail economic belt. The tourism industry has benefited particularly significantly, with notable growth in tourism revenue for small and medium-sized cities along the high-speed rail lines. High-speed rail freight services are also being explored, offering rapid transportation for high-value-added goods. The research and development of 400 km/h-class high-speed maglev trains has also entered the testing and verification phase.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and collaboration to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have created a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening of technological iteration and model innovation, related sectors are expected to embrace faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society hold high expectations for this.
