China's Cross-Border E-Commerce Trade Volume Hits New High

Data released by the Ministry of Commerce shows that China's cross-border e-commerce import and export volume reached 2.8 trillion yuan in 2025, a year-on-year increase of 32%. Exports are dominated by clothing, shoes, and hats, electronics, and household items, while imports are primarily beauty and skincare products, maternal and infant products, and food and health supplements. Growth in emerging markets such as Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Latin America has been particularly significant. The number of overseas warehouses has exceeded 2,500, covering major consumer markets worldwide. Cross-border e-commerce is becoming an important new engine for foreign trade growth.

The rapid development of cross-border e-commerce also benefits from the continuous improvement of logistics infrastructure and the enhancement of international payment systems. Companies such as Cainiao Network and JD Logistics have established efficient sorting and distribution networks overseas, reducing the delivery time for cross-border parcels from two weeks to three to five days. Cross-border payment platforms support multi-currency settlement and localized payment methods. Comprehensive cross-border e-commerce pilot zones have expanded to 165, yielding a number of replicable and scalable institutional innovation achievements in areas such as customs clearance, taxation, and settlement.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening advancement of technological iteration and business model innovation, related fields are expected to usher in faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society hold high expectations for this.
