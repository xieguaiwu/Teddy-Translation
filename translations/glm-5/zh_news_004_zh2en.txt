Total Number of New Energy Vehicles in China Exceeds 40 Million

The latest data from the Ministry of Public Security shows that as of the end of May 2026, the total number of new energy vehicles in China reached 41.5 million, accounting for 12.7% of the country's total vehicle fleet. In the first five months of 2026, newly registered new energy vehicles reached 4.2 million, a year-on-year increase of 45%. In terms of charging infrastructure, the total number of chargers nationwide reached 12 million, optimizing the vehicle-to-charger ratio to 3.5:1. Industry insiders believe that with advancements in battery technology and the improvement of the charging network, the market penetration rate of new energy vehicles will exceed 30% in 2027.

The rapid development of the new energy vehicle industry has also driven the coordinated progress of the upstream and downstream industry chains. The energy density of power batteries continues to increase, and the costs of lithium iron phosphate and ternary material batteries have dropped by approximately 60% and 50%, respectively. Research and development of next-generation battery technologies, such as solid-state batteries and sodium-ion batteries, is also accelerating. The integrated application of intelligent connected technologies has significantly improved the user experience of new energy vehicles in areas such as autonomous driving and smart cabins. The deployment of charging and battery-swapping infrastructure is extending into highway service areas, townships, and communities.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening advancement of technological iteration and business model innovation, related fields are expected to experience even faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are highly anticipating this.
