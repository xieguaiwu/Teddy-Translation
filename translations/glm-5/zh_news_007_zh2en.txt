Urban Renewal Initiatives Drive Renovation of Old Residential Communities

According to the latest briefing from the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, renovation projects have commenced in 280,000 old urban residential communities nationwide, benefiting over 65 million households. The plan for 2026 is to renovate 53,000 old residential communities, with a focus on installing elevators, upgrading water, electricity, gas, and heating networks, and adding parking spaces and charging stations. Various regions are also exploring new models to introduce social capital into the renovation and operation processes. Experts point out that urban renewal is not only a project for people's well-being but also an important avenue for boosting domestic demand and promoting economic growth.

During the renovation of old residential communities, various regions are also exploring the establishment of long-term management mechanisms. Upon completion of renovations, communities are ensuring the sustainability of these improvements by introducing professional property management, establishing homeowners' associations, and setting up maintenance funds. Integrating smart community development with the renovation of old residential communities has promoted the widespread adoption of facilities such as smart access control, smart parking, and smart waste sorting. Resident satisfaction surveys indicate that the living comfort in renovated communities has increased by an average of over 40%.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts note that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and collaboration to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening of technological iteration and model innovation, related sectors are expected to experience faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society are highly anticipating this.
