World's Largest Offshore Wind Power Project Connected to Grid

The world's largest offshore wind power project, undertaken by Chinese enterprises, has achieved full-capacity grid connection in the waters off Yangjiang, Guangdong. The project has a total installed capacity of 6.5 GW and an annual power generation of approximately 26 billion kWh, capable of meeting the annual electricity demand of around 9 million households. The project utilized multiple models of domestically produced large-capacity wind turbines, which reduced construction costs. As a crucial component of clean energy, offshore wind power is becoming a key pillar supporting the transformation of China's energy structure.

The construction of offshore wind power projects has also driven the export of related equipment manufacturing and engineering technical services. Chinese-made wind turbines and installation vessels have been exported to multiple countries and regions, including Europe and Southeast Asia. Integrated development models combining offshore wind power with marine ranching, seawater hydrogen production, and wave power generation are also being explored. Breakthroughs have been made in deep-water and far-offshore floating wind power technology, laying the foundation for the development of wind energy resources in deeper and more distant waters. The rapid development of the offshore wind power industry provides crucial support for China to achieve its carbon peaking and carbon neutrality goals.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and collaboration to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening advancement of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to witness more rapid and high-quality development. All sectors of society hold high expectations for this.
