Significant Progress Made in Deep-Sea Exploration Technology

The China Ocean Mineral Resources Research and Development Association announced that the Fendouzhe manned submersible completed its 100th dive mission in the Mariana Trench in the Pacific Ocean, reaching a maximum depth of 10,909 meters. At the same time, domestically developed deep-sea equipment, such as deep-sea drilling vessels and seafloor observation networks, is also making steady progress. The technological reserves for the exploration and development of deep-sea mineral resources have reached the global forefront. Scientists state that deep-sea research holds irreplaceable scientific value for understanding Earth's evolution, climate change, and the origin of life.

The progress in deep-sea exploration technology has also provided new means for marine resource development and marine environmental protection. Deep-sea mineral resources mainly include polymetallic nodules, cobalt-rich crusts, and hydrothermal sulfides, which contain strategic metal elements such as nickel, cobalt, copper, and manganese. China holds multiple exclusive exploration contract areas in the International Seabed Area. Research on deep-sea ecological environments is also progressing simultaneously, with scientists using deep-sea submersibles to conduct long-term observations of unique ecosystems such as hydrothermal vents and cold seeps, discovering a large number of new species.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that the relevant changes will have a profound impact on the industry's development landscape. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The continuous optimization of policies and positive market feedback have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the further advancement of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to experience more rapid and high-quality development. All sectors of society hold high expectations for this.
