New Curriculum Standards for Compulsory Education Fully Implemented

The Ministry of Education announced that starting from the fall semester of 2026, the new compulsory education curriculum standards will be fully implemented in primary and secondary schools nationwide. Oriented towards core competencies, the new curriculum standards increase the weighting of science education, labor education, and art education. Specifically, the starting grade for science classes has been adjusted from the third grade to the first grade, and the share of class hours for Physical Education and Health has been increased to 10% to 11%. Textbook compilation and teacher training are now fully underway, expected to cover approximately 160 million primary and secondary school students.

The advancement of compulsory education reform also faces challenges such as balanced regional development and the development of the teaching workforce. School conditions in the central and western regions as well as rural areas are continuously improving, and shortages of teachers in key subjects have been addressed through policies such as the Special Post Plan and the Silver Age Teaching Plan. The strategic action for educational digitalization has promoted the collaborative development and sharing of high-quality educational resources. The continued implementation of the "Double Reduction" policy has alleviated students' academic burdens and the burden of off-campus tutoring. Reforms in educational evaluation are also exploring the establishment of a more scientific and diversified evaluation system.

This trend has sparked widespread attention and discussion. Industry experts point out that these changes will have a profound impact on the landscape of industry development. In the long run, all parties need to strengthen coordination and cooperation to jointly address challenges and seize opportunities. The ongoing optimization of policies and the positive feedback from the market have formed a virtuous cycle. In the coming years, with the deepening of technological iteration and model innovation, related fields are expected to experience faster and higher-quality development. All sectors of society hold high expectations for this.
