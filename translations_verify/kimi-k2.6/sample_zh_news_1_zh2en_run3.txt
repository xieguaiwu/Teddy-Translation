Data released by China's National Bureau of Statistics on Wednesday showed that gross domestic product grew 5.4% year on year in the fourth quarter of 2025, with full-year economic expansion at 5.0%. The pace was in line with the government's target range set at the beginning of the year. Economists said recovering consumption and resilient exports were the main growth drivers, though the continued adjustment in the real estate market still exerted downward pressure on the economy. Looking ahead to 2026, analysts expect policy to remain accommodative.
