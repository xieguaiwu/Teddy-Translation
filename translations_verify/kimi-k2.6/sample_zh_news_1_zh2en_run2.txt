Data released by China's National Bureau of Statistics on Wednesday showed that gross domestic product grew 5.4% year on year in the fourth quarter of 2025, while the economy expanded 5.0% for the full year. The growth rate was in line with the target range set by the government at the beginning of the year. Economists said a recovery in consumption and resilient exports were the main drivers, but the ongoing adjustment in the real estate market continued to exert downward pressure on the economy. Looking ahead to 2026, analysts expect policy to remain accommodative.
