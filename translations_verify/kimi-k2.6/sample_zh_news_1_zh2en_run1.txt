Data released by China's National Bureau of Statistics on Wednesday showed that gross domestic product expanded 5.4% year on year in the fourth quarter of 2025, bringing full-year growth to 5.0%. The pace matched the target range set by the government at the beginning of the year. Economists said recovering consumption and resilient exports were the main growth drivers, but the continued adjustment in the real estate market still posed downward pressure on the economy. Looking ahead to 2026, analysts expect policy to remain accommodative.
