The train raced through the darkness, lights outside the window streaking past like meteors. The carriage smelled of instant noodles and cheap perfume. The old man across from him had already fallen asleep, his head resting against the glass, his mouth slightly open. He checked his watch: 3:17 a.m. There were still four hours before the train would arrive. He thought of the bag of oranges his mother had pressed into his hands before he left, still sitting on the luggage rack.
