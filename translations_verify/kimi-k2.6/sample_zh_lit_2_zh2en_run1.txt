The train raced through the darkness; lights outside the window streaked past like shooting stars. The carriage was filled with the smell of instant noodles and cheap perfume. The old man across from him was already asleep, his head resting against the glass, mouth slightly open. He checked his watch: 3:17 a.m. Four more hours until arrival. He thought of the bag of oranges his mother had pressed into his hands before he left, still sitting on the luggage rack.
