The train raced through the darkness, lights outside the window streaking past like shooting stars. The carriage was filled with the smell of instant noodles and cheap perfume. The old man opposite had already fallen asleep, his head leaning against the glass, his mouth slightly open. He looked at his watch: 3:17 in the morning. There were still four hours until arrival. He remembered the bag of oranges his mother had stuffed into his hands as he left, still sitting on the luggage rack.
