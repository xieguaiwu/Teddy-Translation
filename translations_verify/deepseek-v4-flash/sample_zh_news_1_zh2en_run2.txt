Data released by China's National Bureau of Statistics on Wednesday showed that GDP grew 5.4% year-on-year in the fourth quarter of 2025, and full-year economic growth was 5.0%. This growth rate was within the target range set by the government at the beginning of the year. Economists said that consumption recovery and export resilience were the main drivers, but the ongoing adjustment in the real estate market continued to exert downward pressure on the economy. Looking ahead to 2026, analysts expect policies to remain accommodative.
