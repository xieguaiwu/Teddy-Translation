Data released by China's National Bureau of Statistics on Wednesday showed that the gross domestic product (GDP) grew by 5.4% year-on-year in the fourth quarter of 2025, bringing the annual economic growth to 5.0%. This growth rate was within the target range set by the government at the beginning of the year. Economists said that the recovery of consumption and the resilience of exports were the main driving factors, but the continued adjustment in the real estate market still poses downward pressure on the economy. Looking ahead to 2026, analysts expect that policies will continue to maintain an accommodative stance.
