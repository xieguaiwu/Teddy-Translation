Data released by the National Bureau of Statistics of China on Wednesday showed that the gross domestic product grew by 5.4% year-on-year in the fourth quarter of 2025, and the full-year economic growth was 5.0%. This growth rate is within the target range set by the government at the beginning of the year. Economists said that consumption recovery and export resilience are the main driving factors, but the ongoing adjustment of the real estate market still exerts downward pressure on the economy. Looking ahead to 2026, analysts expect policies to continue maintaining a loose stance.
