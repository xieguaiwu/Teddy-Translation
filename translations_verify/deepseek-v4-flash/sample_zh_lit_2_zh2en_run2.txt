The train hurtled through the darkness, lights outside the window streaking past like shooting stars. The carriage was filled with the smell of instant noodles and cheap perfume. The old man opposite had already fallen asleep, his head resting against the window, his mouth slightly open. He looked at his watch. It was 3:17 in the morning. There were still four hours until the train arrived. He thought of the bag of oranges his mother had pressed into his hands before he left, still sitting on the luggage rack.
