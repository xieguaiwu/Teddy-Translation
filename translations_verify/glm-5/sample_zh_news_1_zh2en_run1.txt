Data released by China's National Bureau of Statistics on Wednesday showed that gross domestic product grew 5.4% year-on-year in the fourth quarter of 2025, with full-year economic growth of 5.0%. This growth rate is in line with the target range set by the government at the beginning of the year. Economists said that a recovery in consumption and resilience in exports were the main driving factors, but the ongoing adjustment in the real estate market continues to exert downward pressure on the economy. Looking ahead to 2026, analysts expect policy to maintain an accommodative tone.
