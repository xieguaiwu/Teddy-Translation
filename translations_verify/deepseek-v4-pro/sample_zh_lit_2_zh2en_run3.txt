The train speeds through the darkness, the lights outside the window streaking past like shooting stars. The carriage is thick with the scent of instant noodles and cheap perfume. The old man across from him has already fallen asleep, his head leaning against the glass, his mouth slightly ajar. He glances at his watch: 3:17 a.m. Still four hours until they reach the station. He thinks of the bag of oranges his mother pressed into his hands as he was leaving; it still sits on the luggage rack now.
