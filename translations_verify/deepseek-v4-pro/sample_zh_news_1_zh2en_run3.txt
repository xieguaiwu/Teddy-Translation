Data released by China's National Bureau of Statistics on Wednesday showed that in the fourth quarter of 2025, gross domestic product grew by 5.4% year-on-year, bringing full-year economic growth to 5.0%. This growth rate is in line with the target range set by the government at the beginning of the year. Economists say that consumption recovery and export resilience were the main driving factors, but the ongoing adjustment in the real estate market continues to exert downward pressure on the economy. Looking ahead to 2026, analysts expect policies to maintain an accommodative stance.
