Data released by China's National Bureau of Statistics on Wednesday showed that gross domestic product grew by 5.4% year-on-year in the fourth quarter of 2025, with the economy expanding by 5.0% for the full year. This growth rate was in line with the government's target range set at the beginning of the year. Economists said that consumption recovery and export resilience were the main driving factors, but the ongoing adjustment in the real estate market continued to exert downward pressure on the economy. Looking ahead to 2026, analysts expect policies to maintain an accommodative stance.
