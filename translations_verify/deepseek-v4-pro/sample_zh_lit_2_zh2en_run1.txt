The train raced through the darkness, lights outside the window streaking past like shooting stars. The carriage was thick with the smell of instant noodles and cheap perfume. The old man across from him had already dozed off, head resting against the glass, mouth slightly ajar. He checked his watch: 3:17 a.m. Still four hours until arrival. He remembered the bag of oranges his mother had pressed on him when he left—now still sitting on the luggage rack.
