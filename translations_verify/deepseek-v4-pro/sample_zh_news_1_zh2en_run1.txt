Data released by China's National Bureau of Statistics on Wednesday showed that gross domestic product in the fourth quarter of 2025 grew by 5.4% year-on-year, and the economy expanded by 5.0% for the full year. This growth rate aligns with the government's target range set at the beginning of the year. Economists say consumption recovery and export resilience were the main drivers, but the ongoing adjustment in the real estate market continues to exert downward pressure on the economy. Looking ahead to 2026, analysts expect policies to maintain a generally accommodative stance.
