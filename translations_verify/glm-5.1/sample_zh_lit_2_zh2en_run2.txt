The train sped through the darkness, the lights outside the window streaking past like shooting stars. The air in the carriage was thick with the smell of instant noodles and cheap perfume. The old man across from him was already asleep, his head resting against the glass, his mouth slightly open. He looked at his watch: 3:17 a.m. It would be another four hours before he reached the station. He thought of the bag of oranges his mother had pressed on him before he left, still sitting on the luggage rack.
