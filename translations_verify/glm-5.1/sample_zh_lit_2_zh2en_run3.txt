The train sped through the darkness, the lights outside flashing by like meteors. The carriage was filled with the smell of instant noodles and cheap perfume. The old man opposite him had fallen asleep, his head resting against the glass, his mouth slightly open. He looked at his watch: 3:17 a.m. There were still four hours until they reached the station. He thought of the bag of oranges his mother had pressed into his hands just before he left, still sitting on the luggage rack.
