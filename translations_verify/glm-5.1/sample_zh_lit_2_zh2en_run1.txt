The train sped through the darkness, the lights outside the window streaking past like shooting stars. The carriage was filled with the smell of instant noodles and cheap perfume. The old man opposite had already fallen asleep, his head resting against the glass, his mouth slightly open. He looked at his watch: 3:17 AM. It would be another four hours before they reached the station. He thought of the bag of oranges his mother had pressed into his hands just before he left, still sitting on the luggage rack.
