Electric Aviation Takes Flight with First Commercial Routes

Heart Aerospace announced the launch of the world's first commercial electric aircraft routes, connecting short-haul destinations in Scandinavia. The 30-seat aircraft, powered entirely by batteries, has a range of 200 kilometers and produces zero emissions during flight. The company plans to expand to longer routes as battery technology improves. Several major airlines have placed orders for the aircraft.

The electric aircraft, named ES-30, uses a 1.2 megawatt-hour battery pack that can be recharged in under 30 minutes. The aircraft's electric motors produce significantly less noise than conventional turboprop engines, making it suitable for operations at noise-sensitive urban airports. Heart Aerospace has received orders from several major airlines, including Scandinavian Airlines, United Airlines, and Air Canada. The company is developing a larger, 70-seat version with a 400-kilometer range, expected to enter service by 2030.
