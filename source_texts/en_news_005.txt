First Commercial Space Station Begins Operations

Axiom Space announced that its commercial space station module, Axiom Station, has achieved full operational status and is now hosting its first commercial research crew. The station, attached to the International Space Station, will eventually detach to become a free-flying commercial outpost. Initial tenants include pharmaceutical companies conducting protein crystallization experiments and materials science researchers.

The success of Axiom Station marks a significant milestone in the commercialization of low Earth orbit. The station's modules are designed to be reconfigured for different purposes, from microgravity manufacturing to biological research. Axiom plans to add two additional modules in the coming year, creating a facility with more habitable volume than the ISS. NASA has committed to purchasing crew and cargo services from Axiom through 2035, ensuring a stable revenue stream for the company.
